Canto 4 — Mādhurya-khaṇḍa

The Garga Saṁhitā is an ancient Vaiṣṇava scripture that lovingly narrates the divine life and pastimes of Śrī Kṛṣṇa across seventeen cantos (7 available in English).

Inspired by the divine guidance of Śrī Nārada Muni, the sage Garga Muni, revered as the priest of the Yadu dynasty, born to Paurṇamāsī-devī, undertook austerities and penances before composing this sacred work. The result is a treasured scripture that offers readers a deeper glimpse into the glories of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and the devotional traditions surrounding His transcendental pastimes.

This English translation invites readers to explore the timeless spiritual wisdom, devotional sweetness, and divine narratives preserved within the Garga Saṁhitā.

Chapter 1 - The Story of the Personified Vedas

asatī-kusumopameya-kāntir
yamunā-kūla-kadamba-madhya-vartī
nava-gopa-vadhū-vilāsa-śālī
vana-mālī vitanotu maṅgalāni 4.1.1

“Glorious as an atasi flower, garlanded with forest flowers, and enjoying pastimes with the youthful gopīs in a kadamba forest by the Yamunā’s shore, may Lord Kṛṣṇa grant auspiciousness to us.”

parikarī-kṛta-pīta-paṭaṁ hariṁ
śikhi-kirīṭa-nati-kṛta-kandharam
lakuṭa-veṇu-karaṁ cala-kuṇḍalaṁ
paṭutaraṁ naṭa-veṣa-dharaṁ bhaje 4.1.2

“Dressed as a dancer, with a yellow sash, a peacock-feather crown, swinging earrings, a graceful neck, and a stick and flute in His hand, Lord Kṛṣṇa is the object of my worship.”

śrī-bahulāśva uvāca
śruti-rūpādayo gopyo
bhūta-pūrvā varān mune
kathaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇacandreṇa
jātāḥ pūrṇa-manorathāḥ 4.1.3

Srī Bahulāśva said: “O sage, how did Srī Kṛṣṇacandra fulfill the desires of the personified Vedas that were blessed to take birth as gopīs?”

gopāla-kṛṣṇa-caritaṁ
pavitraṁ paramādbhutam
etad vada mahā-buddhe
tvaṁ parāvara-vittamaḥ 4.1.4

“O wise one, you are the best of they who know everything. Please tell this sacred and wonderful pastime of Lord Gopāla Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
śruti-rūpāś ca yā gopyo
gopānāṁ su-kule vraje
lebhire janma vaideha
śeṣaśāyī-varāc chrutāt 4.1.5

Srī Nārada said: “O king of Videha, because of a benediction given by Lord Seṣaśayī Viṣṇu, some personified Vedas took birth as gopīs in the sainty gopa families of Vraja.”

kamanīyaṁ nanda-sutaṁ
vīkṣya vṛndāvane ca tāḥ
vṛndāvaneśvarīṁ vṛndāṁ
lebhire tad-varecchayā 4.1.6

“In Vṛndāvana forest seeing handsome Kṛṣṇa, and yearning to attain Him, they approached Vṛndā-devī, the ruler of Vṛndāvana.”

vṛndā-dattād varād āśu
prasanno bhagavān hariḥ
nityaṁ tāsāṁ gṛhe yāti
rāsārthaṁ bhakta-vatsalaḥ 4.1.7

“Pleased with Vṛndā’s benediction, Lord Kṛṣṇa, who is always kind to His devotees, every day entered the gopīs homes to enjoy the rāsa dance.”

ekadā tu niśīthinyā
vyatīte prahara-dvaye
rāsārthaṁ bhagavān kṛṣṇaḥ
prāptavāṁs tad-gṛhān nṛpa 4.1.8

“O king, once, when six hours of the night had already passsed, Lord Kṛṣṇa came, very late, to their homes.”

tadā utkaṇṭhitā gopyaḥ
kṛtvā tat-pūjanaṁ param
papracchuḥ parayā bhaktyā
girā madhurayā prabhum 4.1.9

“The anxious gopīs worshiped Him and with sweet words asked a question.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
kathaṁ na cāgataḥ śīghraṁ
no gṛhān vṛjinārdana
utkaṇṭhitānāṁ gopīnāṁ
tvayi candre cakora-vat 4.1.10

The gopīs said: “O savior from troubles, why did You not come sooner? As cakora birds yearn to see the moon, we gopīs always yearn to see You.”

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
yo yasya citte vasati
na sa dūre kadācana
khe sūryaṁ kamalaṁ bhūmau
dṛṣṭvedaṁ sphuṭati priyāḥ 4.1.11

The Supreme Lord said: “O My beloveds, a person who lives in someone’s heart is never far away. Gazing at the sun in the sky, a lotus on the earth is inspired to blossom wide.”

bhāṇḍīre me guruḥ sākṣād
durvāsā bhagavān muniḥ
āgato ’dya priyās tasya
sevārthaṁ gatavān aham 4.1.12

“O My beloveds, today My spiritual master, Lord Durvāsā Muni, came to Bhāṇḍīravana forest, and I went to serve him.”

gurur brahmā gurur viṣṇur
guru devo maheśvaraḥ
guruḥ sākṣāt paraṁ brahma
tasmai śrī-gurave namaḥ 4.1.13

“The spiritual master is like Lord Brahmā. The spiritual master is like Lord Viṣṇu. The spiritual master is like Lord Siva. The spiritual master is like the Supreme Personality of Godhead. I offer my respectful obeisances to my glorious spiritual master.”

ajñāna-timirāndhasya
jñānāñjana-śalākayā
cakṣur unmīlitam yena
tasmai śrī-gurave namaḥ 4.1.14

“I was born in the darkest ignorance, and My spiritual master opened my eyes with the torch of knowledge. I offer My respectful obeisances to him *.”

sva-guruṁ māṁ vijanīyān
nāvamanyeta karhicit
na martya-buddhyā seveta
sarva-deva-mayo guruḥ 4.1.15

“One should know the spiritual master as Myself and never disrespect him in any way. One should not envy him, thinking him an ordinary man, for he is the representative of all the demigods *.”

tasmāt tat-pūjanaṁ kṛtvā
natvā tat-pāda-paṅkajam
āgato ’haṁ vilambena
bhavatīnāṁ gṛhān priyaḥ 4.1.16

“O My beloveds, I worshiped him and offered respects to his lotus feet. That is why I was late in coming to your homes.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
śrutvā tat paramaṁ vākyaṁ
gopyaḥ sarvās tu vismitāḥ
kṛtāñjali-puṭā ūcuḥ
śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ namra-kandharāḥ 4.1.17

Srī Nārada said: “Filled with wonder to hear these words, with bowed heads and folded hands the gopīs spoke to Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
paripūrṇatamasyāpi
durvāsās te guruḥ smṛtaḥ
aho tad-darśanaṁ kartuṁ
mano naś codyataṁ prabho 4.1.18

The gopīs said: “You are the perfect Supreme Personality of Godhead. Durvāsā Muni is Your spiritual master! O Lord, our hearts yearn to see him.”

adya deva niśīthinyā
vyatīte prahara-dvaye
kathaṁ tad-darśanaṁ bhūyād
asmākaṁ parameśvara 4.1.19

“O Supreme Lord, how can we see him now that six hours of the night have passed?”

tathā madhye dīrgha-nadī
yamunā pratibandhikā
kathaṁ tat-taraṇaṁ navaṁ
ṛte deva bhaviṣyati 4.1.20

“The great Yamunā river is a great obstacle. O Lord, how can we cross it without a boat?”

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
avaśyam eva gantavyaṁ
bhavatībhir yadā priyāḥ
yamunām etya caitad vai
vaktavyaṁ mārga-hetave 4.1.21

The Supreme Lord said: “O My beloveds, you must go to see him. When you come to the Yamunā, speak these words:”

yadi kṛṣṇo bāla-yatiḥ
sarva-doṣa-vivarjitaḥ
tarhi no dehi mārgaṁ vai
kālindi saritāṁ vare 4.1.22

“O Yamunā, O best of rivers, if Kṛṣṇa is a perfect and faultess brahmacārī, then please give us a path to cross you.”

ity ukte vacane kṛṣṇā
mārgaṁ vo dāsyati svataḥ
sukhena tena vrajata
yūyaṁ sarvā vrajāṅganāḥ 4.1.23

“When you speak these words, the Yamunā will give you a path. O girls of Vraja, then you may easily cross.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
iti śrutvātha tad-vākyaṁ
pātrair dīrghair vrajāṅganāḥ
ṣaṭ-pañcāśattamān bhogān
nītvā sarvāḥ pṛthak pṛthak 4.1.24

“Hearing these words, the girls of Vraja filled fifty-six great pots with many different kinds of foods.”

yamunām etya hary-uktaṁ
jagur ānata-kandharāḥ
sadyaḥ kṛṣṇa dadau mārgaṁ
gopībhyo maithileśvara 4.1.25

“O king of Mithilā, they went to the Yamunā and with bowed heads repeated Kṛṣṇa’s words. The Yamunā at once gave a path to the gopīs.”

tena gopyo gatāḥ sarvā
bhāṇḍīraṁ cāti-vismitāḥ
tataḥ pradakṣiṇī-kṛtya
muniṁ durvāsasaṁ ca tāḥ
natvā tad-darśanaṁ cakruḥ
puro dhṛtvāśanaṁ bahu
me pūrvaṁ cāpi me pūrvaṁ
annaṁ bhojyaṁ tvayā mune 4.1.26-27

“Filled with wonder, the gopīs went to Bhāṇḍīravana forest. where they saw Durvāsā Muni, bowed down before him, circumambulated him, and, each of them saying, “O sage, the food I have brought should be tasted first,” placed many foods before him.”

evaṁ vivadamānānāṁ
gopīnāṁ bhakti-lakṣaṇam
vijñāya muni-śārdūlaḥ
provāca vimalaṁ vacaḥ 4.1.28

“Understanding the pure devotion of the quarreling gopīs, Durvāsā, the tiger of sages, spoke sweetly to them.”

śrī-munir uvāca
gopyaḥ paramahāṁso ’haṁ
kṛta-kṛtyo hi niṣkriyaḥ
tasmān mukhe me dātavyaṁ
svaṁ svaṁ cāpy aśanaṁ karaiḥ 4.1.29

The sage said: “O gopīs, I am a paramahaṁsa. I have attained the highest goal in life. I never do any material action. For this reason each of you should with her own hand place in my mouth the food she has brought.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
evaṁ vidārite tena
mukhe gopyo ’ti-harṣitāḥ
ṣaṭ-pañcāśattamān bhogān
svān svān sarvāḥ samākṣipan 4.1.30

Srī Nārada said: “The jubilant gopīs then placed in his wide-open mouth all the fifty-six kinds of food they had brought.”

kṣipantīnāṁ ca gopīnāṁ
paśyantīnāṁ munīśvaraḥ
jaghāsa koṭiśo bharān
bhogān sarvān kṣudāturaḥ 4.1.31

“As the gopīs fed him and watched what he did, Durvāsā, the king of sages, ate thousands of bhāras of food as if he were tormented with hunger.”

vismitānāṁ ca gopīnāṁ
paśyantīnāṁ parasparam
itthaṁ śūnyāni pātrāṇi
babhūvur nṛpa-sattama 4.1.32

“O best of kings, when all the pots were emptied the gopīs looked at each other with astonishment.”

atha gopyo muniṁ śāntaṁ
natvā taṁ bhakta-vatsalam
vismitāḥ praṇatāḥ prāhuḥ
sarvāḥ pūrṇa-manorathāḥ 4.1.33

“The astonished gopīs. their desires now fulfilled, bowed down before the peaceful sage, who was always kind to the devotees, and spoke.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
mune āgamanāt pūrvaṁ
kṛṣṇokta-vacasā nadīm
tīrtvāgatās tvat-samīpaṁ
darśanārthaṁ śubhecchayā 4.1.34

The gopīs said: “O sage, when we came here to see you, we were able to cross the river by repeating some words Kṛṣṇa told us.”

itaḥ kathaṁ gamiṣyāmaḥ
sandeho ’yaṁ mahān abhūt
tad vidhehi namas tubhyaṁ
yena panthā laghur bhavet 4.1.35

“How will we return? Now we are filled with doubt. We offer our respects to you. Please give us a way to cross the Yamunā.”

śrī-munir uvāca
sukhenātaḥ pragantavyaṁ
bhavatībhir yadā svataḥ
yamunām etya caitad vai
vaktavyaṁ mārga-hetave 4.1.36

The sage said: “i will give you something to say that will give you easy passage across the Yamunā.”

yadi durvāsasaṁ pītvā
durvāsāḥ kevalaṁ kṣitau
vratī niranno nirvārī
vartate pṛthivī-tale
tarhi no dehi mārgaṁ vai
kālindi saritāṁ vare
ity ukte vacane kṛṣṇā
mārgaṁ vo dāsyati svataḥ 4.1.37-38

“O Yamunā, O best of rivers, if Durvāsā Muni carefully follows his vow of living only by drinking dūrvā grass juice and never eats or drinks anything else, then please give us a path to cross you. When you speak these words, the Yamunā will give you a path to cross her.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
iti śrutvā vaco gopyo
natvā taṁ muni-puṅgavam
yamunām etya muny-uktaṁ
coktvā tīrtvā nadīṁ nṛpa 4.1.39

Srī Nārada said: “O king, hearing these words, the gopīs bowed down before the great sage, went to the Yamunā, repeated the sage’s words, and easily crossed the river.”

śrī-kṛṣṇa-pārśvam ājagmur
vismitā maṅgalāyanāḥ 4.1.40

“Astonished, the saintly gopīs approached Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

atha rāse gopa-vadhvaḥ
sandehaṁ manasotthitam
papracchuḥ śrī-hariṁ vīkṣya
rahaḥ pūrṇa-manorathāḥ 4.1.41

“Then a doubt rose in the gopīs’ hearts. During the rāsa dance, in a secluded place, when their desires were all fulfilled, the gopīs asked a question of Srī Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
durvāsaso darśanaṁ bhoḥ
kṛtam asmābhir agrataḥ
yuvayor vākyataś cātra
sandeho ’yaṁ prajāyate 4.1.42

The gopīs said: “We saw Durvāsā Muni, but now a doubt about what You and he told us has risen in our hearts.”

yathā gurus tathā śiṣyo
mṛṣā-vādī na saṁśayaḥ
jāras tvam asi gopīnāṁ
rasiko bālyataḥ prabho
kathaṁ bāla-yatis tvaṁ vai
vada tad-vṛjinārdana
kathaṁ durvāsasaṁ pītvā
durvāsā bahu-bhuṅ muniḥ
no jāta eṣa sandehaḥ
paśyantīnāṁ vrajeśvara 4.1.43-44

“As the spiritual master acts, so the disciple follows. If the spiritual master speaks lies, then the disciple will also speak lies. O Lord, You are a rake. You are the gopīs’ lover. Since childhood you have enjoyed nectar pastimes with the gopīs. How are You a brahmacārī? O savior from troubles, please tell us. How is Durvāsā a sage that lives only by drinking dūrvā-grass juice? We personally saw him eat an enormous feast. O master of Vraja, this is our doubt.”

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ
samānaḥ sarvagaḥ paraḥ
sadā vaiṣamya-rahito
nirguṇo ’haṁ na saṁśayaḥ 4.1.45

The Supreme Lord said: “I am the Supreme Personality of Godhead. i am all-pervading, faultless, and equal to all. I am free of the material defects of possessiveness and false-ego. I am free of the modes of material nature.”

tathāpi bhaktān bhajato
bhaje ’haṁ vai yathā tathā
tathaiva sādhur jñānī vai
vaiṣamya-rahitaḥ sadā 4.1.46

“As My devotees serve Me, I also serve them in reciprocation. As I reciprocate in this way, so a wise and faultless devotee also reciprocates.”

na buddhi-bhedaṁ janayed
ajñānāṁ karma-saṅginām
joṣayet sarva-karmāṇi
vidvān yuktaḥ samācaran 4.1.47

“Let not the wise disrupt the minds of the ignorant who are attached to fruitive action. They should not be encouraged to refrain from work, but to engage in work in the spirit of devotion *.”

yasya sarve samārambhāḥ
kāma-saṅkalpa-varjitāḥ
jñānāgni-dagdha-karmāṇaṁ
tam āhuḥ paṇḍitaṁ budhāḥ 4.1.48

“One is understood to be in full knowledge whose every act is devoid of desire for sense gratification. He is said by sages to be a worker whose fruitive action is burned up by the fire of perfect knowledge *.”

nirāśir yata-cittātmā
tyakta-sarva-parigrahaḥ
śarīraṁ kevalaṁ karma
kurvan nāpnoti kilbiṣam 4.1.49

“Such a man of understanding acts with mind and intelligence perfectly controlled, gives up all sense of proprietorship over his possessions and acts only for the bare necessities of life. Thus working, he is not affected by sinful reactions. *”

na hi jñānena sādṛśaṁ
pavitram iha vidyate
tat svayaṁ yoga-saṁsiddhaḥ
kālenātmani vindati 4.1.50

“In this world, there is nothing so sublime and pure as transcendental knowledge. Such knowledge is the mature fruit of all mysticsm. And one who has achieved this enjoys the self within himself in due course of time *.”

brahmaṇy ādhāya karmāṇi
saṅgaṁ tyaktvā karoti yaḥ
lipyate na sa pāpena
padma-patram ivāmbhasā 4.1.51

“One who performs his duty without attachment, surrendering the results unto the Supreme God, is not affected by sinful action, as the lotus leaf is untouched by water *.”

tasmān munis tu durvāsāḥ
bahu-bhuk tvad-dhite rataḥ
na tasya bhojanecchā syād
dūrvā-rasa-mitāśanaḥ 4.1.52

“Durvāsā Muni ate that great feast for your sake. He did not desire to eat it. He lives by drinking a little dūrvā-grass juice.”

Chapter 2 - The Story of the Gopīs That Had Been Sages

śrī-nārada uvāca
gopīnāṁ ṛṣi-rūpāṇāṁ
ākhyānaṁ śṛṇu maithila
sarva-pāpa-haraṁ puṇyaṁ
kṛṣṇa-bhakti-vivardhanam 4.2.1

Srī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, please hear the story of the gopīs that had been sages, a sacred story that removes all sins and increases one’s love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

baṅgeṣu maṅgalo nāma
gopa āsīn mahā-manāḥ
lakṣmivān chruta-sampanno
nava-lakṣa-gavāṁ patiḥ 4.2.2

“In east Bengal lived a noble-hearted, learned, and wealthy gopa named Maṅgala. He was the master of nine-hundred-thousand cows.”

bhāryāḥ pañca sahasrāṇi
babhūvus tasya maithila
kadācid daiva-yogena
dhanaṁ sarvaṁ kṣayaṁ gatam 4.2.3

“O king of Mithilā, he had five thousand wives. One day all his wealth was destroyed by fate.”

corair nītās tasya gāvaḥ
kāścid rājñā hṛtā balāt
evaṁ dainye ca samprāpte
duḥkhito maṅgalo ’bhavat 4.2.4

“Some of his cows were stolen by thieves and the rest were forcibly taken by the king. Now a pauper, Maṅgala was filled with sorrow.”

tadā śrī-rāmasya varād
daṇḍakāraṇya-vāsinaḥ
ṛṣayaḥ strītvam āpannā
babhūvus tasya kanyakāḥ 4.2.5

“Ths sages of Daṇḍakāraṇya, who by Lord Rāmacandra’s blessing were destined to become gopīs, became the daughters of Maṅgala.”

dṛṣṭvā kanyā-samūhaṁ sa
duḥkhī gopo ’tha maṅgalaḥ
uvāca dainya-duḥkhāḍhya
ādhi-vyādhi-samākulāḥ 4.2.6

“When he saw his many daughters, Maṅgala-gopa became filled with sorrow. Miserable in his poverty and tormented with headaches and diseases, Maṅgala spoke.”

śrī-maṅgala uvāca
kiṁ karomi kva gacchāmi
ko me dukhaṁ vyapohati
śrīr na bhūtir nābhijano na balaṁ me ’sti sāmpratam 4.2.7

Srī Maṅgala said: “What shall I do? Where shall I go? Who will take away my sufferings? Now I have no wealth, no opulences, no dynasty, and no strength.”

dhanaṁ vinā kathaṁ cāsāṁ
vivāho hā bhaviṣyati
bhojane yatra sandeho
dhanāśā tatra kīdṛśī 4.2.8

“How will my daughters marry if I have no wealth? If even the next meal is uncertain, where is the hope to gain wealth?”

sati dainye kanyakāḥ syuḥ
kāka-tālīyavad gṛhe
tasmāt kasyāpi rājñas tu
dhanino balinas tv aham
dāsyāmy etāḥ kanyakāś ca
kanyānāṁ saukhya-hetave
kadarthī-kṛtya tāḥ kanyā
evaṁ buddhyā sthito ’bhavat
tadaiva mathurād deśād
gopaś caikaḥ samāgataḥ 4.2.9-10

“Now that I am suddenly poor, how will I make my daughters happy by marrying them to wealthy and powerful kings? As Maṅgala was thinking in this way, a gopa from Mathurā district came to his home.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
tīrtha-yāyī jayo nāma
vṛddho buddhimatāṁ varaḥ
tan-mukhān nanda-rājasya
śrutaṁ vaibhavam adbhutam 4.2.11

Srī Nārada said: “From an elderly pilgrim named Jaya, who was the wisest of the wise, Maṅgala had heard of the fabulous wealth of King Nanda.”

nanda-rājasya valaye
maṅgalo dainya-pīḍitāḥ
vicintya preṣayām āsa
kanyakāś cāru-locanāḥ 4.2.12

“After some thought, the pauper Maṅgala sent his beautiful-eyed daughters to King Nanda’s circle.”

tā nanda-rājasya gṛhe
kanyakā ratna-bhūṣitāḥ
gavāṁ gomaya-hāriṇyo
babhūvur go-vrajeṣu ca 4.2.13

“In King Nanda’s home the girls, now decorated with jewels, milked the cows in the barns.”

śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ sundaraṁ dṛṣṭvā
kanyā jāti-smarāś ca tāḥ
kālindī-sevanaṁ cakrur
nityaṁ śrī-kṛṣṇa-hetave 4.2.14

“Gazing at handsome Srī Kṛṣṇa, and remembering their previous birth, the girls daily worshiped the Yamunā to attain Kṛṣṇa.”

athaikadā śyāmalāṅgī
kālindī dīrgha-locanā
tābhyaḥ sva-darśanaṁ dattvā
varaṁ dātuṁ samudyatā 4.2.15

“One day the large-eyed and dark-complexioned goddess Yamunā appeared before them and offered to give them a benediction.”

tā vavrire vrajeśasya
putro bhūyāt patiś ca naḥ
tathāstu coktvā kālindī
tatraivāntaradhīyata 4.2.16

“They said, “May Srī Kṛṣṇa, the son of Vraja’s king, become our husband.” Yamunā replied, “So be it,” and suddenly disappeared.”

Chapter 3 - The Story of the Mithilā Women

śrī-nārada uvāca
maithilīnāṁ goipikānāṁ
ākhyānaṁ śṛṇu maithila
daśāśvamedha-tīrthasya
phaladaṁ bhakti-vardhanam 4.3.1

Srī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, please hear the story of the Mithilā women that become gopīs, a story that brings the result of visiting sacred Daśāśvamedha-tīrtha, a story the increases one’s love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-rāmasya varāj jātā
nava-nanda-gṛheṣu yāḥ
kāmanīyaṁ nanda-sūnuṁ
dṛṣṭvā tā moham āsthitāḥ 4.3.2

“Because of Lord Rāmacandra’s benediction they took birth in the homes of the nine Nandas. When they saw handsome Kṛṣṇa, they became bewildered with love for Him.”

mārgaśīrṣe śubhe māsi
cakruḥ kātyāyanī-vratam
upacāraiḥ ṣoḍaśabhiḥ
kṛtvā devīṁ mahī-mayīm 4.3.3

“During the auspicious month of Mārgaśīrṣa (November-December) they observed a vow to worship goddess Kātyāyanī. They made a clay deity of the goddess and worshiped her with sixteen offerings.”

aruṇodaya-velāyāṁ
snātāḥ śrī-yamunā-jale
nityaṁ sametā ājagmur
gāyantyo bhagavad-guṇān 4.3.4

“Singing Lord Kṛṣṇa’s glories as they went, every morning they would bathe in the Yamunā’s waters.”

ekadā tāḥ sva-vastrāṇi
tīre nyasya vrajāṅganāḥ
vijahrur yamunā-toye
karābhyāṁ siñcatīr mithaḥ 4.3.5

“Once, placing their garments on the riverbank, the girls of Vraja, played in the Yamunā’s waters, splashing each other with both hands.”

tāsāṁ vāsāṁsi sannīya
bhagavān prātar āgataḥ
tvaraṁ kadambam āruhya
cauravan maunam aṣṭhitaḥ 4.3.6

“Coming there that morning, Lord Kṛṣṇa silently stole their garments and climbed a kadamba tree.”

tā na vīkṣya sva-vāsāṁsi
vismitā gopa-kanyakāḥ
nīpa-sthitaṁ vilokyātha
sa-lajjā jahasur nṛpa 4.3.7

“Not seeing their clothes, the gopīs were surprised. O king, when they saw Kṛṣṇa in the tree they shyly smiled.”

pratīcchantu sva-vāsāṁsi
sarvā āgatya cātra vai
anyathā na hi dāsyāmi
vṛkṣāt kṛṣṇa uvāca ha 4.3.8

From the tree Kṛṣṇa said: “All who wish their garments, come here and take them. If you do not come, I will not give them.”

rājantyas tāḥ śīta-jale
hasantyaḥ prāhur ānatāḥ 4.3.9

“Bowing their heads as they stood in the cold water, the smiling girls spoke.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
he nanda-nandana manohara gopa-ratna
gopāla-vaṁśa-nava-haṁsa mahārti-hārin
śrī-śyāmasundara tavoditam adya vākyaṁ
kūrmaḥ kathaṁ vivasanāḥ kila te ’pi dāsyaḥ 4.3.10

The gopīs said: “O handsome son of Nanda, O jewel of the gopas, O young swan in the gopa dynasty, O destroyer of sufferings, O handsome dark one, how can we follow Your order? We are Your maidservants, but we have no clothes.”

gopāṅganā-vasana-muṇ navanīta-hārī
jāto vraje ’ti-rasikaḥ kila nirbhayo ’si
vāsāṁsi dehi na hi cen mathurādhipāya
vakṣyāmahe ’nayam atīva kṛtaṁ tvayātra 4.3.11

“You are a thief of butter and thief of gopīs’ garments. You are a fearless rake in the village of Vraja. Give us our garments. If You don’t we will tell King Kaṁsa, the ruler of Mathurā, the evil You have done here.”

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
dāsyo mamaiva yadi sundara-manda-hāsā
icchantu vaitya kila cātra kadamba-mūle
no cet samasta-vasanāni nayāmi gehāṁs
tasmāt kariṣyatha mamaiva vaco ’vilambāt 4.3.12

The Blessed Lord said: “O sweetly-smiling girls, if you actually are My maidservants, and if you actually want your garments, then come to the roots of this kadamba tree. If you do not come, I will bring all these garments to your homes. Because I will do that you should follow My orders without delay.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
tadā tā nirgatāḥ sarvā
jalād gopyo ’ti-vepitāḥ
ānatā yonim acchādya
pāṇibhyāṁ śīta-karṣitāḥ 4.3.13

Srī Nārada said: “Shivering with the cold, bowing their heads, and covering their pubic areas with both hands, all the gopīs emerged from the water.”

kṛṣṇa-dattāni vāsāṁsi
dadhuḥ sarvā vrajāṅganāḥ
mohitāś ca sthitās tatra
kṛṣṇe lajjāyitekṣaṇāḥ 4.3.14

“Taking their garments from Kṛṣṇa, all the girls of Vraja dressed themselves. Bewildered with love, they shyly gazed at Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

jñātvā tāsām abhiprāyaṁ
parama-prema-lakṣaṇam
āha manda-smitaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ
samantād vīkṣya tā vacaḥ 4.3.15

“Aware of the great love in their hearts, Kṛṣṇa gently smiled. Placing His eyes on all of them, He spoke.”

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
bhavatībhir mārgaśīrṣe
kṛtaṁ kātyāyanī-vratam
mad-arthaṁ tac ca sa-phalaṁ
bhaviṣyati na saṁśayaḥ 4.3.16

The Blessed Lord said: “To attain Me, during the month of Mārgaśīrṣa you followed a vow to worship goddess Kātyāyanī. Your vow will bear its fruit. There is no doubt of that.”

paraśvo ’hani cāṭavyāṁ
kṛṣṇā-tīre manohare
yuṣmābhiś ca kariṣyāmi
rāsaṁ pūrṇa-manoratham 4.3.17

“The day after tomorrow, on the Yamunā’s beautiful shore, I will enjoy with You a rāsa dance that will fulfill all your desires.”

Chapter 4 - The Story of the Kauśalā Women

śrī-nārada uvāca
kauśalānāṁ gopikānāṁ
varṇanaṁ śṛṇu maithila
sarva-pāpa-haraṁ puṇyaṁ
śrī-kṛṣṇa-caritāmṛtam 4.4.1

Srī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, please hear the story of the Kauśala womwn that became gopīs, a sacred story that removes all sins and is filled with the nectar of Lord Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.”

navopananda-geheṣu
jātā rāma-varād vraje
pariṇītā gopa-janaiḥ
ratna-bhūṣaṇa-bhūṣitāḥ 4.4.2

“By Lord Rāmacandra’s blessing they were born in the homes of the nine Upanandas. Decorated with jewel ornaments, they were married to various gopas.”

pūrṇa-candra-pratīkāśā
nava-yauvana-samyutāḥ
padminyo haṁsa-gamanāḥ
padma-patra-vilocanāḥ 4.4.3

“They were young, glorious as full moons, delicate as lotus flowers, and graceful as swans. Their eyes were like lotus petals.”

jāra-dharmeṇa su-snehaṁ
su-dṛḍhaṁ sarvato ’dhikam
cakruḥ kṛṣṇe nanda-sute
kamanīye mahātmani 4.4.4

“They had fallen passionately in love with Nanda’s handsome son Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead. They loved Him as a paramour.”

tābhiḥ sārdhaṁ sadā hāsyaṁ
vraja-vīthiṣu mādhavaḥ
smitaiḥ pīta-paṭādānaiḥ
karṣaṇaiḥ sa cakāra ha 4.4.5

“Smiling, and tugging at their yellow garments, Kṛṣṇa teased them and joked with them on the pathways of Vraja.”

dadhi-vikrayārthaṁ yāntyas tāḥ
kṛṣṇa kṛṣṇeti cābruvan
kṛṣṇe hi prema-samyuktā
bhramantyaḥ kuñja-maṇḍale 4.4.6

“In the market selling yogurt, they did not call out, Yogurt! Yogurt!” Instead they called “Kṛṣṇa! Kṛṣṇa!” Overcome with love for Kṛṣṇa, they wandered the forests searching for Him.”

khe vāyau cāgni-jalayor
mahyāṁ jyotir diśāsu ca
drumeṣu jana-vṛndeṣu
tāsāṁ kṛṣṇo hi lakṣyate 4.4.7

“When they looked at the different objects of the creation made of ether, air, fire, and water, when they looked in the different directions, when they looked at the luminaries in the sky, when they looked at trees, and when they looked at people, they did not see them. They only saw Kṛṣṇa.”

prema-lakṣaṇa-samyuktāḥ
śrī-kṛṣṇa-hṛta-mānasāḥ
aṣṭabhiḥ sāttvikair bhāvaiḥ
sampannās tāś ca yoṣitaḥ 4.4.8

“They were filled with love for Kṛṣṇa. Kṛṣṇa had stolen their hearts. They could not help but display many symptoms of love for Him.”

premṇā paramahaṁsānāṁ
padavīṁ samupāgatāḥ
kṛṣṇānandāḥ pradhāvantyo
vraja-vīthiṣu tā nṛpa 4.4.9

“Filled with love, they followed the path of the paramahaṁsas. Filled with bliss, they ran along Vraja’s pathways.”

jaḍā jaḍaṁ na janantyo
jaḍonmatta-piśāca-vāt
abruvantyo bruvantyo vā
gata-lajjā gata-vyathāḥ 4.4.10

“Sometimes they were stunned, sometimes they could not understand that inanimate objects were actually not alive, sometimes they were silent, and sometimes they spoke on and on without care or shyness.”

evaṁ kṛtārthatāṁ prāptas
tan-mayā yāś ca gopikāḥ
balād ākṛṣya kṛṣṇasya
cucumbur mukha-paṅkajam 4.4.11

“Rapt in thinking of Kṛṣṇa, they attained the highest goal of life. Irresistibly attracted to Kṛṣṇa, they could not help but kiss His lotus lips.”

tāsāṁ tapaḥ kiṁ kathayāmi rājan
pūrṇe pare brahmaṇi vāsudeve
yāś cakrire prema hṛd-indriyādyair
visṛjya loka-vyavahāra-mārgam 4.4.12

“O king, how can I describe the austerities they must have performed? Abandoning the path of what is ordinarily thought desireable in this world, with all their heart and senses they loved Lord Kṛṣṇa, the Supreme Personality of Godhead.”

yā rāsa-raṅge vinidhāya bāhuṁ
kṛṣṇāṁsayoḥ prema-vibhinna-cittāḥ
cakrur vaśe kṛṣṇam alaṁ tapas tad
vaktuṁ na śakto vadanaiḥ phaṇīndraḥ 4.4.13

“Overcome with love, and placing an arm around His shoulders in the rāsa dance, they made Kṛṣṇa their surrendered servant. Even Lord Seṣa with His many mouths cannot describe the auterities they must have performed.”

yogena saṅkhyena śubhena karmaṇā
nyāyādi-vaiśeṣika-tattva-vittamaiḥ
yat prāpyate tac ca padaṁ videha-rāṭ
samprāpyate kevala-bhakti-bhāvataḥ 4.4.14

“O king of Videha, whatever good result may be attained by yoga, saṅkhya, good deeds, or the study of logic and philosophy, is at once attained simply by loving Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

Chapter 5 - The Story of the Ayodhyā Women

śrī-nārada uvāca
ayodhyā-vāsinīnāṁ tu
gopīnāṁ varṇanaṁ śṛṇu
catuṣpadārtha-daṁ sākṣāt
kṛṣṇa-prāpti-karaṁ param 4.5.1

Srī Nārada said: “Please hear the story of the Ayaodhyā women that became gopīs, a story that grants the four goals of life and brings the company of Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

sindhu-deśeṣu nagarī
campakā nāma maithila
babhūva tasyāṁ vimalo
rājā dharma-parāyaṇaḥ 4.5.2

“O king of Mithilā, in Sindhu-deśa there was a city named Campakā. The saintly king there was Vimala.”

kuvera iva kosāḍhyo
mānasvī mṛgarāḍ iva
viṣṇu-bhaktaḥ praśāntātmā
prahlāda iva mūrtimān 4.5.3

“Like Kuvera he was wealthy. Like a lion he was noble and chivalrous. Like Prahlāda he was a peaceful-hearted devotee of Lord Viṣṇu.”

bhāryānāṁ ṣaṭ-sahasrāṇi
babhūvus tasya bhū-pateḥ
rūpavatyaḥ kañja-netrā
vandhyatvaṁ tāḥ samāgatāḥ 4.5.4

“However, King Vimala’s six thousand beautiful, lotus-eyed wives were all barren.”

apatyaṁ kena puṇyena
bhūyān me ’tra śubhaṁ nṛpa
evaṁ cintayatas tasya
bahavo vatsarā gatāḥ 4.5.5

“He spent many years wondering, “What pious deed with bring me a child?”

ekadā yājñavalkyas tu
munīndras tam upāgataḥ
taṁ natvābhyarcya vidhivan
nṛpas tat-sammukhe sthitaḥ 4.5.6

“One day Yājñavalkya, the king of sages, visited. The king bowed down, worshiped him, and stood respectfully before him.”

cintākulaṁ nṛpaṁ vīkṣya
yājñavalkyo mahā-muniḥ
sarva-jñaḥ sarva-vic chāntaḥ
pratyuvāca nṛpottamam 4.5.7

“Seeing the king was very anxious, the peaceful and all-knowing sage Yājñavalkya spoke.”

śrī-yājñavalkya uvāca
rājan kṛśo ’si kasmāt tvaṁ
kā cintā te hṛdi sthitā
saptasv aṅgeṣu kuśalaṁ
dṛśyate sāmprataṁ tava 4.5.8

Srī Yājñavalkya said: “O king, why are you distraught? What is the worry in your heart? I can see the seven limbs of your body are marked with all auspiciousness.”

śrī-vimala uvāca
brahmaṁs tvaṁ kiṁ na jānāsi
tapasā divya-cakṣuṣā
tathāpy ahaṁ vadiṣyāmi
bhavato vākya-gocarāt 4.5.9

Srī Vimala said: “O brāhmaṇa, what do you not know? Because you have spiritual eyes attained by great austerities, you know everything. Still I will tell you my worry.”

ānapatyena duḥkhena
vyāpto ’haṁ muni-sattama
kiṁ karomi tapo dānaṁ
vada yena bhavet prajā 4.5.10

“Because I have no child I am filled with grief. O best of sages, please describe the austerity or charity that will bring me a child.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
iti śrutvā yājñavalkyo
dhyāna-stimita-locanaḥ
dīrghaṁ dadhyau muni-śreṣṭho
bhūtaṁ bhavyaṁ vicintayan 4.5.11

Srī Nārada said: “Hearing this, the great sage Yāj 24avalkya for a long time closed his eyes. Meditating in trance, he looked into the past and future.”

śrī-yājñavalkya uvāca
asmin janmani rājendra
putro naiva ca naiva ca
putryas tava bhaviṣyanti
koṭiśo nṛpa-sattama 4.5.12

Srī Yājñavalkya said: “O best of kings, in this birth you have no son. No son at all. You will have ten million daughters.”

śrī-rājovāca
putraṁ vinā pūrva-ṛṇān na ko ’pi
pramucyate bhūmi-tale munīndra
sadā hy aputrasya gṛhe vyathā syāt
paraṁ tv ihāmutra sukhaṁ na kiñcit 4.5.13

The king said: “O king of sages, without a son no one in this world can become free of his many debts (to the sages, demigods, and forefathers) and become liberated. Without a son there is always trouble at home. Without a son there can be no happiness in either this life or the next.”

śrī-yājñavalkya uvāca
mā khedaṁ kuru rājendra
putryo deyās tvayā khalu
śrī-kṛṣṇāya bhaviṣyāya
paraṁ dāyādikaiḥ saha 4.5.14

Srī Yājñavalkya said: “O king, don’t be unhappy. With a great dowry you will give all your daughters in marriage to Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

tenaiva karmaṇā tvaṁ vai
devarṣi-pitṛṇāṁ ṛṇāt
vimukto nṛpa-śārdūla
paraṁ mokṣam avāpsyasi 4.5.15

“O tiger of kings, by doing that you will be free of your debts to the demigods, sages, and ancestors, and you will attain liberation.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
tadāti-harṣito rājā
śrutvā vākyaṁ mahā-muneḥ
punaḥ papraccha sandehaṁ
yājñavalkyaṁ mahā-munim 4.5.16

Srī Nārada said: “Very happy to hear these words, the king asked Yājñavalkya Muni the following question.”

śrī-rājovāca
kasmin kule kutra deśe
bhaviṣyaḥ śrī-hariḥ svayam
kīdṛg rūpaś ca kiṁ varṇo
varṣaiś ca katibhir gataiḥ 4.5.17

The king said: “In what country will Lord Kṛṣṇa appear? In what family? What will be His form? What will be His caste? How many years hence will He come?”

śrī-yājñavalkya uvāca
dvāparasya yugasyāsya
tava rājyān mahā-bhuja
avaśeṣe varṣa-śate
tathā pañca-daśe nṛpa
tasmin varṣe yadu-kule
mathurāyāṁ yadoḥ pure
bhādre budhe kṛṣṇa-pakṣe
dhātrarkṣe harṣane vṛṣe
vave ’ṣṭamyām ardha-rātre
nakṣatreśa-mahodaye
andhakārāvṛte kāle
devakyāṁ śauri-mandire
bhaviṣyati hariḥ sākṣād
araṇyām adhvare ’gni-vat
śrīvatsāṅko ghana-śyāmo
vana-māly-ati-sundaraḥ 4.5.18-21

Srī Yājñavalkya said: “O mighty-armed king, in the Dvāpara-yuga, when you have reigned for 115 years, in this (Bhārata) varṣa, in Mathurā, the city of the Yadus, in the Yadu family, during the month of Bhādra (August-September), on a Monday that is the eighth-day of the dark-moon, in the middle of a very dark night, just as the moon is rising, when Taurus and the star Rohiṇī are prominent, and when the stars are in harṣaṇa-yoga, in Vasudeva’s palace, handsome Lord Kṛṣṇa, dark as a monsoon cloud, decorated with a forest garland, and marked with Srīvatsa, will appear in Devakī’s womb as fire appears in a yajña’s araṇi sticks.”

Chapter 6 - The Story of the Ayodhyā Women

śrī-nārada uvāca
evam uktvā gate sākṣād
yājñavalkye mahā-munau
atīva harṣam āpanno
vimalaś campakā-patiḥ 4.6.1

Srī Nārada said: “Then the great sage Yājñavalkya left. Vimala, the king of Campakā, was very happy.”

ayodhyā-pura-vāsinyaḥ
śrī-rāmasya varāc ca yāḥ
babhūvus tasya bhāryāsu
tāḥ sarvāḥ kanyakāḥ śubhāḥ 4.6.2

“Then the Ayodhyā women blessed by Lord Rāmacandra took birth as the very beautiful daughters of Vimala and his wives.”

vivāha-yogyās tā dṛṣṭvā
cintayaṁś campakā-patiḥ
yājñavalkya-vacaḥ smṛtvā
dūtam āha nṛpeśvaraḥ 4.6.3

“Seeing that his daughters had come to a marriagable age, and remembering Yājñavalkya’s words, King Vimala spoke.”

śrī-vimala uvāca
mathurāṁ gaccha dūta tvaṁ
gatvā śauri-gṛhaṁ śubham
darśanīyās tvayā putro
vasudevasya sundaraḥ 4.6.4

Srī Vimala said: “O messenger, go to Mathurā, enter Vasudeva’s palace, and look for Vasudeva’s handsome son.”

śrīvatsāṅko ghana-śyāmo
vana-mālī catur-bhujaḥ
yadi syāt tarhi dāsyāmi
tasmai sarvāḥ su-kanyakāḥ 4.6.5

“He is dark as a monsoon cloud, has four arms, is marked with Srīvatsa, and wears a forest garland. If He is there, I will give Him all my beautiful daughters.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
iti vākyaṁ tataḥ śrutvā
dūto ’sau mathurāṁ gataḥ
papraccha sarvābhiprāyaṁ
māthurāṁś ca mahājanān 4.6.6

Srī Nārada said: “Hearing these words, and going to Mathurā, the messenger asked the great souls there if they had seen Vasudeva’s son.”

tad-vākyaṁ māthurāḥ śrutvā
kaṁsa-bhītāḥ su-buddhayaḥ
taṁ dūtaṁ rahasi prāhuḥ
karṇānte manda-vāg yathā 4.6.7

“Hearing these words, the intelligent people of Mathurā, who were all afraid of Kaṁsa, took him to a secluded place and whispered in his ear.”

śrī-māthurā ūcuḥ
vasudevasya ye putrāḥ
kaṁsena bahavo hatāḥ
ekāvaśiṣṭāvarajā
kanyā sāpi divaṁ gatā 4.6.8

The people of Mathurā said: “Vasudeva’s many sons were all killed by Kaṁsa. Only a daughter, who went to the heavenly planets, survived.”

vasudevo ’sti cātraiva
hy aputro dīna-mānasaḥ
idaṁ na kathanīyaṁ hi
tvayā kaṁsa-bhayaṁ pure 4.6.9

āmalakī tataḥ paścān
nāmnā vai pāpa-mocanī
kāmadā ca tataḥ paścāt
kathitā vai varūthinī 4.8.14

“. . . 8. āmalakī, 9. pāpa-mocanī, 10. kāmadā, 11. varūthinī, . . .”

mohinī cāparā proktā
nirjalā kathitā tataḥ
yoginī deva-śayanī
kāminī ca tataḥ param 4.8.15

“. . . 12. mohinī, 13. aparā, 14. nirjalā, 15. yoginī, 16. deva-śayanī, 17. kāminī, . . .”

pavitrā cāpy ajā padmā
indirā ca tataḥ param
pāśāṅkuśā ramā caiva
tataḥ paścāt prabodhinī 4.8.16

“. . . 18. pavitrā, 19. ajā, 20. padmā, 21. indirā, 22. pāśāṅkuśā, 23. ramā, 24. prabodhinī.”

sarva-sampat-pradā caiva
dve prokte malamāsa-je
evaṁ sad-viṁśatiṁ nāmnāṁ
ekādaśyāḥ paṭhec ca yaḥ
samvatsara-dvādaśīnāṁ
phalam āpnoti so ’pi hi 4.8.17

“There are also two more ekādaśīs, both named sarva-sampat-pradā, during the extra month of leap-year. In this way there are twenty-six ekādaśīs in all. A person who chants the names of these twenty-six ekādaśīs attains the result of following ekādaśī for one year.”

ekādaśyāś ca niyamaṁ
śṛṇutātha vrajāṅganāḥ
bhūmi-śāyī daśamyāṁ tu
caika-bhukto jitendriyaḥ 4.8.18

“O girls of Vraja, please hear the rules for observing ekādaśī. On ekādaśī one should control the senses and sleep on the ground. On dvādaśī one should eat only once.”

eka-vāraṁ jalaṁ pītvā
dhauta-vastro ’ti-nirmalaḥ
brāhme muhūrta utthāya
caikādaśyāṁ hariṁ nataḥ 4.8.19

“During ekādaśī one should be pure-hearted and very clean, wear clean garments, drink water only once, rise for brāhma-muhūrta, and bow down to Lord Kṛṣṇa”

adhamaṁ kūpika-snānaṁ
vāpyāṁ snānaṁ tu madhyamam
taḍāge cottamaṁ snānaṁ
nadyāḥ snānaṁ tataḥ param 4.8.20

“Bathing with well-water is an inferior kind of bath. Bathing in a pond is better, bathing in a lake is better than that, and bathing in a river is better still.”

evaṁ snātvā nara-varaḥ
krodha-lobha-vivarjitaḥ
nālapet tad-dine nīcāṁs
tathā pākhaṇḍino narān 4.8.21

“In this way one should bathe. On ekādaśī day one should be free of greed and anger, and one should not talk to sinful people, atheists, and offenders.”

mithyā-vāda-ratāṁś caiva
tathā brāhmaṇa-nindakān
anyāṁś caiva durācārān
āgamyāgamane ratān
para-dravyāpahārāṁś ca
para-dārābhigāminaḥ
durvṛttān bhinna-maryādān
nālapet sa vratī naraḥ 4.8.22-23

“A person who follows the vow of ekādaśī should not talk to liers, offenders of brāhmaṇas, sinners, debauchees, thieves, adulterers, and the ill-behaved and immoral.”

keśavaṁ pūjayitvā tu
naivedyaṁ tatra kārayet
dīpaṁ dadyād gṛhe tatra
bhakti-yuktena cetasā 4.8.24

“In the home one should, with devotion in the heart, worship Lord Kṛṣṇa, and offer Him food and a lamp.”

kathāḥ śrutvā brāhmaṇebhyo
dadyāt sad-dakṣiṇāṁ punaḥ
rātrau jāgaraṇaṁ kuryād
gāyan kṛṣṇa-padāni ca 4.8.25

“From the brāhmaṇas one should hear the stories of Lord Kṛṣṇa. One should offer dakṣiṇā to them. One should keep a vigil, singing the glories of Lord Kṛṣṇa throughout the night.”

kāṁsyaṁ māṁsaṁ masūrāṁs ca
kodravaṁ caṇakaṁ tathā
śākaṁ madhu parānnaṁ ca
punar bhojana-maithunam
viṣṇu-vrate tu kartavye
daśamyāṁ daśa varjayet
dyūtaṁ krīḍāṁ ca nidrāṁ ca
tāmbūlaṁ danta-dhāvanam 4.8.26-27

“When ekādaśī is ended, and the daśamī day has come, one should avoid these ten things: 1. eating from a brass dish, 2. eating flesh, 3. masūra dāl, 4. kodrava grains, 5. chick-peas, 6. spinach, 7. honey, 8. and the food of others, 9. eating twice, and 10. sex. One should also avoid gambling, playing, sleeping, chewing betelnuts, and brushing the teeth.”

parāpavādaṁ paiśūnyaṁ
steyaṁ hiṁsāṁ tathā ratim
krodhāḍhyaṁ hy anṛtaṁ vākyaṁ
ekādaśyāṁ vivarjayet 4.8.28

“On ekādaśī one should avoid harsh speech, slander, theft, violence, sex, anger, and speaking lies.”

kāṁsyaṁ māṁsaṁ surāṁ kṣaudraṁ
tailaṁ vitathā-bhāṣaṇam
puṣṭi-ṣaṣṭi-masūrāṁś ca
dvādaśyāṁ parivarjayet 4.8.29

“On the dvādaśī one should avoid brass utensils, flesh, liquor, honey, oil, speaking lies, puṣṭi, ṣaṣṭi, and masūra.”

anena vidhinā kuryād
dvādaśī-vratam uttamam 4.8.30

“By following these rules one should observe the great vow of ekādaśī and dvādaśī.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
ekādaśī-vratasyāsya
kālaṁ vada mahā-mate
kiṁ phalaṁ vada tasyās tu
māhātmyaṁ vada tattvataḥ 4.8.31

The gopīs said: “O noble-hearted one, please tell us when the vow of ekādaśī should be observed. What is the result of following ekādaśī? Please tell us. Please tell us in truth the glories of ekādaśī.”

śrī-rādhovāca
daśamī pañca-pañcāśad
ghaṭikā cet pradṛśyate
tarhi caikādaśī tyājyā
dvādaśīṁ samupoṣayet 4.8.32

“If dvādaśī starts within the first twenty-two hours of ekādaśī, one should not fast on ekādaśī, but on dvādaśī instead. Note: One ghaṭikā equals twenty-four minutes. Fifty-five ghaṭikās equal twenty-two hours.”

daśamī phala-mātreṇa
tyājyā caikādaśī tithiḥ
madirā-bindu-pātena
tyājyo gaṅga-ghaṭo yathā 4.8.33

“As one avoids drinking a cup of Gaṅgā water into which a drop of wine has fallen, so one should avoid fasting on such an ekādaśī.”

ekādaśī yadā vṛddhiṁ
dvādaśī ca yadā gatā
tadā parā hy upoṣyā syāt
pūrvā vai dvādaśī-vrate 4.8.34

“When ekādaśī goes to its completion, and dvādaśi arrives at the proper time, then one should fast on ekādaśī.”

ekādaśī-vratasyāsya
phalaṁ vakṣye vrajāṅganāḥ
yasya śravaṇa-mātreṇa
vājapeya-phalaṁ labhet 4.8.35

“O girls of Vraja, now I will tell you the result of following ekādaśī. Simply by hearing this description one attains the reuslt of performing a vājapeya-yajña.”

aṣṭāśīti-sahasrāṇi
dvijān bhojayate tu yaḥ
tat kṛtaṁ phalam āpnoti
dvādaśī-vrata-kṛn naraḥ 4.8.36

“A person who follows the vow of ekādaśī and dvādaśī attains the same pious result one attains by feeding eighty-eight brāhmaṇas.”

sa-sāgara-vanopetāṁ
yo dadāti vasundharām
tat-sahasra-guṇaṁ puṇyaṁ
ekādaśyā mahā-vrate 4.8.37

“A person who follows ekādaśī attains a pious result thousands of times greater than the pious result attained by performing the ritual of mixing the earth and the ocean.”

ye saṁsārārṇave magnāḥ
pāpa-paṅka-samākule
teṣām uddharaṇārthāya
dvādaśī-vratam uttamam 4.8.38

“For they who are drowning in the ocean of repeated birth and death, an ocean muddy with many sins, the vow of fasting on ekādaśī is the best means of deliverance.”

rātrau jāgaraṇaṁ kṛtvai-
kādaśī-vrata-kṛn naraḥ
na paśyati yamaṁ raudraṁ
yuktaḥ pāpa-śatair api 4.8.39

“Even though contaminated with hundreds of sins, a person who keeps a nighttime vigil while following the vow of ekādaśī never sees angry Yamarāja.”

pūjayed yo hariṁ bhaktyā
dvādaśyāṁ tulasī-dalaiḥ
lipyate na sa pāpena
padma-patram ivāmbhasā 4.8.40

“As a lotus leaf is never touched by water, so a person who worships Lord Kṛṣṇa with tulasī leaves on dvādaśī is never touched by sin.”

aśvamedha-sahasrāṇi
rājasūya-śatāni ca
ekādaśy-upavāsasya
kalaṁ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm 4.8.41

“The results of a thousand aśvamedha-yajñas and a hundred rājasūya-yajñas are not equal to even a sixteenth part of the result of fasting on ekādaśī.”

daśa vai mātṛke pakṣe
tathā vai daśa paitṛke
priyayā daśa pakṣe tu
puruṣān uddharen naraḥ 4.8.42

“A person who follows ekādaśī delivers ten generations of his mother’s family, ten generations of his father’s family, and ten generations on his wife’s family.”

yathā śuklā tathā kṛṣṇā
dvayoś ca sādṛśaṁ phalam
dhenuḥ śvetā tathā kṛṣṇā
ubhayoḥ sādṛśaṁ payaḥ 4.8.43

“As a white cow and a black cow both give the same kind of milk, so the ekādaśī of the bright fortnight and the ekādaśī of the dark fortnight both bring the same result.”

meru-mandara-mātrāṇi
pāpāni śata-janmasu
ekaṁ caikādaśīṁ gopyo
dahate tūla-rāśi-vat 4.8.44

“O gopīs, a fire burns a great pile of cotton, so a single ekādaśī burns the great Mount Meru of sins committed in a hundred births.”

vidhivad vidhi-hīnaṁ vā
dvādaśyāṁ dānam eva ca
sv-alpaṁ vā su-kṛtaṁ gopyo
meru-tulyaṁ bhavec ca tat 4.8.45

“O gopīs, when, either following the proper method or not following it, and whether it is great or small, one gives charity on dvādaśī, that charity becomes as great as a Mount Meru of charity.”

ekādaśī-dine viṣṇoḥ
śṛṇute yo hareḥ kathām
sapta-dvīpavatī-dāne
yat phalaṁ labhate ca saḥ 4.8.46

“A person who on ekādaśī hears the stories of Lord Kṛṣṇa attains the same pious result he would attain by giving great charity everywhere on the seven continents.”

śaṅkhoddhāre naraḥ snātvā
dṛṣṭvā devaṁ gadādharam
ekādaśy-upavāsasya
kalāṁ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm 4.8.47

“A person who bathes at Saṅkhoddhara-tīrtha and gazes at the Deity of Lord Gadādhara there does not attain even one sixteenth of the piety one attains by fasting on ekādaśī.”

prabhāse ca kurukṣetre
kedāre badrikāśrame
kāśyāṁ ca śūkara-kṣetre
grahaṇe candra-sūryayoḥ
saṅkrantīnāṁ catur-lakṣaṁ
dānaṁ dattaṁ ca yan naraiḥ
ekādaśy-upavāsasya
kalāṁ nārhanti ṣoḍaśīm 4.8.48-49

“A person who makes four-hundred thousand pilgrimages to Prabhāsa, Kurukṣetra, Kedāra, Badarikāśrama, Vārāṇasī, and Sūkarakṣetra during eclipses of the sun or moon and gives great charity there does not attain even one sixteenth of the piety one attains by fasting on ekādaśī.”

nāgānāṁ ca yathā śeṣaḥ
pakṣiṇāṁ garuḍo yathā
devānāṁ ca yathā viṣṇur
varṇānāṁ brāhmaṇo yathā
vṛkṣāṇāṁ ca yathāśvatthaḥ
patrāṇāṁ tulasī yathā
vratānāṁ ca tathā gopyo
varā caikādaśī tithiḥ 4.8.50

“O gopīs, as Lord Seṣa is the best of serpents, as Garuḍa is the best of birds, as Lord Viṣṇu is the best of Deities, as the brāhmaṇas are the best of castes, as the banyan is the best of trees, and as a tulasī leaf is the best of leaves, so fasting on ekādaśī day is the best of sacred vows.”

daśa-varṣa-sahasrāṇi
tapas tapyati yo naraḥ
tat-tulyaṁ phalam āpnoti
dvādaśī-vrata-kṛn naraḥ 4.8.52

“A person who follows ekādaśī attains the result of performing austerities for ten thousand years.”

Chapter 9 - The Glories of Srī Ekādaśī

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
vṛṣabhānu-sute su-bhru
sarva-śāstrārtha-pāra-ge
viḍambayantī tvaṁ vācā
vācaṁ vācaspater muneḥ 4.9.1

The gopīs said: “O beautiful-eyebrowed daughter of King Vṛṣabhānu, O girl who has traveled to the farther shore of the deep meanings of all the scriptures, Your words are like Bṛhaspati Muni’s words.”

ekādaśī-vrataṁ rādhe
kena kena purā kṛtam
tad brūhi no viśeṣeṇa
tvaṁ sākṣāj jñāna-śevadhiḥ 4.9.2

“O Rādhā, what great souls followed ekādaśī in ancient times? Please tell us. You know all this.”

śrī-rādhovāca
ādau devaiḥ kṛtaṁ gopyo
varam ekādaśī-vratam
bhraṣṭa-rājyasya lābhārthaṁ
daityānāṁ nāśanāya ca 4.9.3-4

Srī Rādhā said: “O gopīs, in the beginning of creation the demigods followed ekādaśī to destroy the demons and regain their lost kingdom.”

vaiśantena purā rājñā
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
sva-pitus taraṇārthāya
yamaloka-gatasya ca 4.9.4

“In ancient times King Viśanta followed ekādaśī to rescue his father from the world of Yamarāja.”

akasmāl lumpakenāpi
jñāti-tyaktena pāpinā
ekādaśī kṛtā yena
rājyaṁ lebhe sa lumpakaḥ 4.9.5

“A sinner named Lumpaka, who abandoned his relatives, followed ekādaśī and attained a great kingdom.”

bhadrāvatyāṁ ketumatā
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
putra-hīnena sad-vākyāt
putraṁ lebhe sa mānavaḥ 4.9.6

“In the city of Bhadrāvatī, King Ketumān was sonless. Following ekādaśī on a great saint’s advice, he obtained a son.”

brāhmaṇyai deva-patnībhir
dattam ekādaśī-vratam
tena lebhe svarga-saukhyaṁ
dhana-dhanyaṁ ca mānuṣī 4.9.7

“Once the demigoddesses gave ekādaśī to a brāhmaṇī. By following ekādaśī that brāhmaṇī attained great wealth and happiness like that on Svargaloka.”

puṣpadantī-mālyavantau
śakra-śāpāt piśācatām
prāptaṁ kṛtaṁ vrataṁ tābhyāṁ
punar gandharvatāṁ gatau 4.9.8

“Cursed by Indra, Puṣpadantī and Mālyavān became demons, but by following ekādaśī they again became Gandharvas.”

purā śrī-rāmacandreṇa
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
samudre setubandhārthaṁ
rāvaṇasya vadhāya ca 4.9.9

“To build a bridge on the ocean and kill Rāvaṇa, in ancient times even Lord Rāmacandra followed ekādaśī.”

layānte ca samutpannā
dhatṛ-vṛkṣa-tale surāḥ
ekādaśī-vrataṁ cakruḥ
sarva-kalyāṇa-hetave 4.9.10

“When, after the period of cosmic devastation had ended, the demigods were again manifested from the tree of creation, they followed ekādaśī to attain all auspiciousness.”

vrataṁ cakāra medhāvī
dvādaśyāḥ pitṛ-vākyataḥ
apsaraḥ-sparśa-doṣena
mukto ’bhūn nirmala-dyutiḥ 4.9.11

“Following ekādaśī on Pitā’s advice, Medhāvī became free from the sin of touching an apsarā and became pure and splendid again.”

gandharvo lalitaḥ patnyā
gataḥ śāpāt sa rakṣatām
ekādaśī-vratenāpi
punar gandharvatāṁ gataḥ 4.9.12

“Transformed into demons by a curse, Lalita and his wife followed ekādaśī and became Gandharvas again.”

ekādaśī-vratenāpi
mandhātā svar-gatiṁ gataḥ
sagaraś ca kakutsthaś ca
mucukundo mahā-matiḥ 4.9.13

“By following ekādaśī, Mandhātā, Sagara, Kakutstha, and noble-hearted Mucukunda attained Svargaloka.”

dhundhumārādayaś cānye
rājāno bahavas tathā
brahma-kapāla-nirmukto
babhūva bhagavān bhavaḥ 4.9.14

“By following ekādaśī Dhundhumāra and many other kings also attained Svargaloka. By following ekādaśī Lord Siva was able to put down his necklace of brāhmaṇas’ skulls.”

dhṛṣṭabuddhir vaiśya-putro
jñāti-tyakto mahā-khalaḥ
ekādaśī-vrataṁ kṛtvā
vaikuṇṭhaṁ sa jagāma ha 4.9.15

“The sinner Dhṛṣṭabuddhi, who was a vaiśya’s son, and who abandoned his own relatives, followed ekādaśī and went to Vaikuṇṭha.”

rājñā rukmāṅgadenāpi
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
tena bhu-maṇḍalaṁ bhuktvā
vaikuṇṭhaṁ sa-puro yayau 4.9.16

“By following ekādaśī King Rukmaṅgada was able to rule the circle of the earth and go, taking with him the citizens of his kingdom, to Vaikuṇṭha.”

ambarīṣeṇa rājñāpi
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
nāspṛśad brahma-śāpo ’pi
yo na pratihataḥ kvacit 4.9.17

“Even though he did nothing to protect himself from it, King Ambarīṣa, a follower of ekādaśī, was untouched by a brāhmaṇa’s curse.”

hemamālī nāma yakṣaḥ
kuṣṭhī dhanada-śāpataḥ
ekādaśī-vrataṁ kṛtvā
candra-tulyo babhūva ha 4.9.18

“A yakṣa named Hemamālī, who became a leper by Kuvera’s curse, followed ekādaśī and became cured. He became splendid as the moon.”

mahījitā nṛpenāpi
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
tena putraṁ śubhaṁ labdhvā
vaikuṇṭhaṁ sa jagāma ha 4.9.19

“By following ekādaśī, King Mahījit attained a good son and then went to Vaikuṇṭha.”

hariścandreṇa rājñāpi
kṛtam ekādaśī-vratam
tena labdhvā mahī-rājyaṁ
vaikuṇṭhaṁ sa-puro yayau 4.9.20

“By following ekādaśī, King Hariścandra attained a great kingdom and, taking all his subjects with him, went to Vaikuṇṭha.”

śrī-śobhano nāma purā kṛte yuge
jāmātṛko ’bhūn mucukunda-bhūbhṛtaḥ
ekādaśīṁ yaḥ samupoṣya bhārate
prāptaḥ sa devaiḥ kila mandarācale 4.9.21 adyāpi rājyaṁ kurute kuvera-vad
rājñā yuto ’sau kila candrabhāgayā
ekādaśīṁ sarva-tithīśvarīṁ paraṁ
janītha gopyo na hi tat-samānyā 4.9.22

“Even today one can attain a kingdom like Kuvera’s simply by following ekādaśī. O gopīs, please know that ekādaśī is the queen of all holy days. No other holy day is her equal.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
iti rādhā-mukhāc chrutvā
yajña-sītāś ca gopikāḥ
ekādaśī-vrataṁ cakrur
vidhivat kṛṣṇa-lālasāḥ 4.9.23

Srī Nārada said: “After hearing from Srī Rādhā’s mouth the glories of ekādaśī, the gopīs that had been yajña-sītās, and who were now yearning to attain Srī Kṛṣṇa, carefully followed ekādaśī.”

Chapter 10 - The Story of the Pulindā Women

śrī-nārada uvaca
pulindakānāṁ gopīnāṁ
kariṣye varṇanaṁ hy ataḥ
sarva-pāpa-haraṁ puṇyaṁ
adbhutaṁ bhakti-vardhanam 4.10.1

Srī Nārada said: “Now I will tell you the story of the Pulindā women that become gopīs, a wonderful and sacred story that removes all sins and increases one’s love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

pulindā udbhaṭāḥ kecid
vindhyādri-vana-vāsinaḥ
vilumpanto rāja-vasu
dīnānāṁ na kadācana 4.10.2

“Living in the forests of the Vindhya Hills, many Pulinda highwaymen stole the kings wealth, although they never stole from the poor.”

kupitas teṣu balavān
vindhyā-deśādhipo balī
akṣauhiṇībhyāṁ tān sarvān
pulindān sa rurodha ha 4.10.3

“Very angry, the powerful king of Vindhyā-deśa took two akṣauhiṇī divisions of soldiers, and confronted all the Pulindas.”

yuyudhas te ’pi khaḍgaiś ca
kuntaiḥ śūlaiḥ paraśvadhaiḥ
śakty-ṛṣṭibhir bhūṣuṇḍībhiḥ
śaraiḥ kati dināni ca 4.10.4

“For how many days did they fight with swords, kunta and śūla spears, battle-axes, śaktis, ṛṣṭis, and bhuṣuṇḍīs?”

patraṁ te preṣayām āsuḥ
kaṁsāya yadu-bhūbhṛte
kaṁsa-praṇodito daityaḥ
pralambo balavāṁs tadā 4.10.5

“To the Yadu-king Kaṁsa the Pulindas sent a letter begging for aid. Kaṁsa sent a powerful demon named Pralamba.”

yojana-dvayam uccāṅgaṁ
kāla-megha-sama-dyutim
kirīṭa-kuṇḍala-dharaṁ
sarpa-hāra-vibhūṣitam 4.10.6

“He was sixteen miles tall, dark as a cloud, decorated with helmet and earrings, and garlanded with a serpent.”

pādayoḥ śṛṅkhalāyuktaṁ
gadā-pāṇiṁ kṛtāntavat
lalaj-jihvaṁ ghora-rūpaṁ
pātayantaṁ girīn drumān 4.10.7

“With chains around his ankles, a club in his hand, a tongue moving to and fro, and a horribly ugly form, he looked like death personified. When he walked he flattened the hills and made the trees fall.”

kampayantaṁ bhuvaṁ vegāt
pralambaṁ yuddha-durmadam
dṛṣṭvā pradarśito rājā
sa-sainyo raṇa-maṇḍalam
tyaktvā dudrāva sahasā
siṁhaṁ vīkṣya gajo yathā
pralambas tān samānīya
mathurām āyayau punaḥ 4.10.8-9

“Staring at Pralamba, who was drunk with the idea of a fight, and who made the earth shake, the king and his army fled the battlefield as an elephant flees when it sees a lion. Then Pralamba took the Pulindas to Mathurā.”

pulindās te ’pi kaṁsasya
bhṛtyatvaṁ samupāgatāḥ
sa-kuṭumbāḥ kāma-girau
vāsaṁ cakrur nṛpeśvara 4.10.10

“O king of kings, the Pulindas and their families all became Kaṁsa’s servants. They lived at Kāmagiri.”

teṣāṁ gṛheṣu sañjātāḥ
śrī-rāmasya varāt parāt
pulindyaḥ kanyakā divyā
rūpiṇyaḥ śrīr ivārcitā 4.10.11

“By Lord Rāmacandra’s blessing many Pulinda women took birth as their splendidly beautiful daughters. They were glorious and worshipable as the goddess of fortune herself.”

tad-darśana-smara-rujaḥ
pulindyaḥ prema-vihvalāḥ
śrīmat-pāda-rajo dhṛtvā
dhyāyantyas tam ahar-niśam 4.10.12

“Simply by seeing Lord Kṛṣṇa, they were overcome with passionate love for Him. They embraced the dust that had touched His feet. They thought of Him day and night.”

tāś cāpi rāse samprāptāḥ
śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ parameśvaram
paripūrṇatamaṁ sākṣād
golokādhipatiṁ prabhum 4.10.13

“During the rāsa dance they attained the association of Lord Kṛṣṇa, the perfect Supreme Personality of Godhead, the ruler of Goloka.”

śrī-kṛṣṇa-caraṇāmbhoja-
rajo devaiḥ su-durlabham
aho bhāgyaṁ pulindīnāṁ
tāsāṁ prāptaṁ viśeṣataḥ 4.10.14

“The great good fortune of these Pulindā girls is that they attained the dust of Srī Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, dust that even the great demigods cannot attain.”

yaḥ pārameṣṭhyam akhilaṁ na mahendra-dhiṣṇyaṁ
no sārvabhaumam aniśaṁ na rasādhipatyam
no yoga-siddhim abhito na punar bhavaṁ vā
vāñchaty alaṁ parama-pāda-rajaḥ sa bhaktaḥ 4.10.15

“A person who does not desire the post of Brahmā, the post of Indra, a great royal kingdom, the kingdom of Pātālaloka, mystic powers, or even freedom from the world of birth and death, but desires only the dust of the Supreme Personality of Godhead’s feet, is a true devotee of the Lord.”

Chapter 11 - The Story of the Gopīs that were Residents of...

śrī-nārada uvāca
anyāsāṁ caiva gopīnāṁ
varṇanaṁ śṛṇu maithila
sarva-pāpa-haraṁ puṇyaṁ
hari-bhakti-vivardhanam 4.11.1

Śrī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, please hear the story of the other gopīs, a sacred story that removes all sins, and increases one’s love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

nīti-vin mārgadaḥ śuklaḥ
pataṅgo divya-vāhanaḥ
gopeṣṭāś ca vraje rājan
jātā ṣaḍ vṛṣabhānavaḥ 4.11.2

“O king, the six Vṛṣabhānus: Nītivit, Mārgada, Śukla, Pataṅga, Divyavāhana, and Gopeṣṭa, were born in Vraja.”

teṣāṁ gṛheṣu sañjātā
lakṣmī-pati-varāt prajāḥ
ramā-vaikuṇṭha-vāsinyaḥ
śrī-sakhyo ’pi samudra-jāḥ
ūrdhvaṁ vaikuṇṭha-vāsinyas
tathājita-padāśritāḥ
śrī-lokācala-vāsinyaḥ
śrī-sakhyo ’pi samudra-jāḥ 4.11.3-4

“In their homes, by a benediction granted by Lord Nārāyaṇa, were born the girls of Ramā-Vaikuṇṭha, Urdhva-Vaikuṇṭha, Lokācala, and Ajitapada, who had all been born from the ocean of milk and who were all friends of the goddess of fortune.”

cintayantyaḥ sadā śrīmad-
govinda-caraṇāmbujam
śrī-kṛṣṇasya prasādārthaṁ
tābhir māgha-vrataṁ kṛtam 4.11.5

“Always thinking of Lord Kṛṣṇa’s lotus feet, to gain His favor they followed the vow of Māgha-vrata.”

māghasya śukla-pañcamyāṁ
vasantādau hariḥ svayam
tāsāṁ prema-parīkṣārthaṁ
kṛṣṇo vai tad-gṛhān gataḥ 4.11.6

“On the fifth day of the bright fortnight of the month of Māgha (January- February), as springtime was about to begin, to test their love for Him, Lord Kṛṣṇa went to these gopīs’ homes”

vyāghra-carmāmbaraṁ bibhran
jaṭa-mukuṭa-maṇḍitaḥ
vibhūti-dhūsaro veṇuṁ
vādayan mohayan jagat 4.11.7

“Dressed in a tigerskin, His hair matted, and His natural opulences concealed, by playing the flute he enchanted all the worlds.”

tāsāṁ vīthiṣu samprāptiṁ
vīkṣya gopyo ’pi sarvataḥ
āyayur darśanaṁ kartuṁ
mohitāḥ prema-vihvalāḥ 4.11.8

“Seeing Him on the path, the enchanted gopīs were overcome with love. To gaze at Him, they came close.”

atīva sundaraṁ dṛṣṭvā
yoginaṁ gopa-kanyakāḥ
ūcuḥ parasparaṁ sarvāḥ
premānanda-samākulāḥ 4.11.9

“As they gazed at this very handsome yogī, the blissful gopīs spoke among themselves.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
ko ’yaṁ śiśur nanda-sutākṛtir vā
kasyāpi putro dhanino nṛpasya
narī-ku-vāg-bāṇa-vibhinna-marmā
jāto virakto gata-kṛtya-karmā 4.11.10

Thwe gopīs said: “Who is this? Is this Nanda’s son or the song of a wealthy king? Is He renounced because His heart was broken by a woman’s harsh words or because He has performed many pious deeds?”

atīva-ramyaḥ sumkumāra-deho
manoja-vad-viśva-mano-haro ’yam
aho kathaṁ jīvati cāsya mātā
pitā ca bhāryā bhaginī vinainam 4.11.11

“He is very handsome. His features are graceful and delicate. Like Kāmadeva, He charms the hearts of the entire world. Ah! How can He live without mother, father, sister, and wife?”

evaṁ tāḥ sarvato yūthī-
bhūtvā sarvā vrajāṅganāḥ
papracchus taṁ yogi-varaṁ
vismitāḥ prema-vihvalāḥ 4.11.12

“Filled with wonder and love, the assembled girls of Vraja asked many questions.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
kas tvaṁ yogin nāma kiṁ te
kutra vāsas tu te mune
kā vṛttis tava kā siddhir
vada no vadataṁ vara 4.11.13

The gopīs said: “O yogī, who are You? What is Your name? O sage, where do You live? What do You do? What mystic powers have You attained? O best of eloquent speakers, please tell us.”

śrī-siddha uvāca
yogeśvaro ’haṁ me vāsaḥ
sadā māna-sarovare
nāmnā svayamprakāśo ’haṁ
nirannaḥ sva-balāt sadā 4.11.14

The perfect yogī said: “I am a master of yoga. I always stay at Māna-sarovara. My name is Svayamprakāśa. I never eat. My own mystic power keeps Me alive.”

sārthe paramahāṁsānāṁ
yāmy ahaṁ te vrajāṅganāḥ
bhūtaṁ bhāvyaṁ vartamānaṁ
vedmy ahaṁ divya-darśanaḥ 4.11.15

“O girls of Vraja, I have attained the great treasure of the paramahaṁsas. I have spiritual eyes. I can see the past, present, and future.”

uccāṭanaṁ māraṇaṁ ca
mohanaṁ stambhanaṁ tathā
janāmi mantra-vidyābhir
vaśī-karaṇam eva ca 4.11.16

“I know mantras that can ruin people, kill them, bewilder them, paralyze them, or bring them under My control.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
yadi jānāsi yogiṁs tvaṁ
vārtāṁ kāla-trayodbhavām
kiṁ vartate no manasi
vada tarhi mahā-mate 4.11.17

Thw gopīs said: “O wise yogī, if You know the past, present, and future, then please tell us one thing: What are we thinking at this moment?”

śrī-siddha uvāca
bhāvatīnāṁ ca karṇānte
kathanīyam idaṁ vacaḥ
yusmad-ājñayā vā vakṣye
sarveṣāṁ śṛṇvatām iha 4.11.18

The perfect yogī said: “The answer to this question I will now whisper in each girl’s ear, or, if you request, I will speak it aloud so all may hear.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
satyaṁ yogeśvaro ’si tvaṁ
tri-kāla-jño na saṁśayaḥ
vaśī-karaṇa-mantreṇa
sadyaḥ paṭhana-mātrataḥ
yadi so ’traiva cāyāti
cintito yo ’sti vai mune
tadā manyāmahe tvāṁ vai
mantriṇāṁ pravaraṁ param 4.11.19-20

The gopīs said: “O sage, if it is true that You are a master of yoga, and if without doubt that You know the past, present, and future, and if by merely chanting a mantra You can actually bring others under Your control, then we think You must be the best of all mantra-chanters.”

śrī-siddha uvāca
durlabho durghaṭo bhāvo
yusmābhir gaditaḥ striyaḥ
tathāpy ahaṁ kariṣyāmi
vākyaṁ na calate satām 4.11.21

The perfect yogī said: “Any impossible thing you ask, I can do. The words of saintly persons do not prove false.”

nimīlayata netrāṇi
mā śocāṁ kuruta striyaḥ
bhaviṣyati na sandeho
yuṣmākaṁ kāryam eva ca 4.11.22

“O girls, don’t lament. Close your eyes and you will attain your wish. Of this there is no doubt.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
tatheti mīlitākṣiṣu
gopīṣu bhagavān hariḥ
vihāya tad-yogi-rūpaṁ
babhau śrī-nanda-nandanaḥ 4.11.23

Śrī Nārada said: “As the gopīs stood with closed eyes, the Lord abandoned the form of a yogī and again became Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda.”

netrāṇy unmīlya dadṛśuḥ
sānandaṁ nanda-nandanam
vismitās tat-prabhāva-jñā
harṣitā moham āgatāḥ 4.11.24

“Opening their eyes, they happily gazed at Kṛṣṇa, the son of Nanda. Delighted, filled with wonder, and now aware of His transcendental powers, the gopīs fainted unconscious.”

Chapter 12 - The Story of the Gopīs That In the Holi Festival Displayed Three Transcendental Virtues

idaṁ mayā te kathitaṁ
gopīnāṁ caritaṁ śubham
anyāsāṁ caiva gopīnāṁ
varṇanaṁ śṛṇu maithila 4.12.1

“O king of Mithilā, now that I have described the beautiful activities of these gopīs, please hear the story of some other gopīs.”

vītihotro ’gnibhuk sāmbaḥ
śrīkaro gopatiḥ śrutaḥ
vrajeśaḥ pāvanaḥ śānta
upanandāḥ vrajebhavāḥ 4.12.2

“Born in Vraja, the Upanandas were: Vītihotra, Agnibhuk, Sāmba, Śrīkara, Gopati, Śruta, Vrajeśa, Pāvana, and Śānta.”

dhanavanto rūpavantaḥ
putravanto bahu-śrutāḥ
śīlādi-guṇa-sampannāḥ
sarve dāna-parāyaṇāḥ 4.12.3

“They were all wealthy, handsome, learned, noble, virtuous, charitable, and the fathers of many children.”

teṣu gṛheṣu sañjātāḥ
kanyakā deva-vākyataḥ
kāścid divyā adivyāś ca
tathā tri-guṇa-vṛttayaḥ 4.12.4

“Following the orders of the demigods, these girls, endowed with three virtues, some from the celestial planets, and some not from the celestial planets, took birth in the homes of the Upanandas.”

bhūmi-gopyaś ca sañjātāḥ
puṇyair nānā-vidhaiḥ kṛtaiḥ
rādhikā-sahacaryās tāḥ
sakhyo ’bhuvan videha-rāṭ 4.12.5

“O king of Videha, having performed many pious deeds, these gopīs were born on the earth and became the friends of Śrī Rādhā.”

ekadā māninīṁ rādhāṁ
tāḥ sarvā vraja-gopikāḥ
ūcur vīkṣya hariṁ prāptaṁ
holikāyā mahotsave 4.12.6

“Noticing that Kṛṣṇa was ready to celebrate the great Holi festival, these gopīs approached Rādhā, who was then filled with jealous anger, and spoke to Her.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
rambhoru candra-vadane madhu-māninīśe
rādhe vacaḥ su-lalitaṁ lalane śṛṇu tvam
śrī-holikotsava-vihāram alaṁ vidhātuṁ
āyāti te pura-vane vraja-bhūṣaṇo ’yam 4.12.7

The gopīs said: “O moon-face, banana-tree thigh, sweet as honey Queen Rādhā, please hear these pleasing words. Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the ornament of Vraja, has come to Your forest to celebrate the Holi festival.”

śrī-yauvanonmada-vighūrṇita-locano ’sau
nīlālakāli-kalitāṁsa-kapola-golaḥ
sat-pīta-kañcuka-ghanāntam aśeṣm ārād
ācālayan dhvani-matā sva-padāruṇena 4.12.8

“His eyes rolling in the intoxication of glorious youth, His black locks resting on His cheeks and shoulders covered with a yellow shawl, He walks here, the anklets tinkling on His reddish feet.”

bālārka-mauli-vimalāṅgada-hāram udyad-
vidyut-kṣipan-makara-kuṇḍalam ādadhānaḥ
pītāmbareṇa jayati dyuti-maṇḍalo ’sau
bhū-maṇḍale sa-dhanuṣeva ghano divi-sthaḥ 4.12.9

“Wearing glittering armlets, yellow garments, a crown like the rising sun, and shark earrings that eclipse lightning, He is splendid and glorious as a monsoon cloud and a rainbow.”

ābīra-kuṅkuma-rasaiś ca vilipta-deho
haste gṛhita-nava-secana-yantra ārāt
prekṣaṁs tavāśu sakhi vāṭam atīva rādhe
tvad-rāsa-raṅga-rasa-keli-rataḥ sthitaḥ saḥ 4.12.10

“Eager to enjoy a rāsa-dance with You, His body anointed with red kunkuma, and a sprinkler in His hand, He fixes His gaze on as You stay nearby in this courtyard-garden.”

nirgaccha phālguna-miṣeṇa vihāya mānaṁ
dātavyam adya ca yaśaḥ kila holikāyai
kartavyam āśu nija-mandira-raṅga-vāri-
pāṭīra-paṅka-makaranda-cayaṁ ca tūrṇam 4.12.11

“Give up Your jealous anger, and celebrate the glorious Holi festival. Go at once and get water mixed with red dye, sandal paste, and flower-pollen.”

uttiṣṭha gaccha sahasā nija-maṇḍalībhir
yatrāsti so ’pi kila tatra mahā-mate tvam
etādṛśo ’pi samayo na kadāpi labhyaḥ
prakṣālitaṁ kara-talaṁ viditaṁ pravāhe 4.12.12

“Stand up! Go at once! O noble-hearted one, with Your friends go to where He is! An opportunity like this will not come again. Wash Your hand in the stream of the Holi festival.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
atha mānavatī rādhā
mānaṁ tyaktvā samutthitā
sakhī-saṅghaiḥ parivṛtā
prakartuṁ holikotsavam 4.12.13

Śrī Nārada said: “Then angry Rādhā put aside Her jealous anger, stood up, and, accompanied by Her friends, went to celebrate the Holi festival.”

śrīkhaṇḍāguru-kastūrī-
haridra-kuṅkuma-dravaiḥ
pūritābhir dṛtībhiś ca
samyuktās tā vrajāṅganāḥ 4.12.14

“The girls of Vraja carried sprinklers filled with water mixed with sandal, aguru, musk, yellow turmeric, and red kuṅkuma.”

rakta-hastāḥ pīta-vastrāḥ
kūjan-nūpura-mekhalāḥ
gāyantyo holikā-gītir
galībhir hāsya-sandhibhiḥ 4.12.15

“With red hands and yellow garments, and with rowdy songs and laughter, they sang about the glories of Holi.”

ābīrāruṇa-cūrṇānāṁ
muṣṭibhis tā itas tataḥ
kurvatyaś cāruṇaṁ bhūmiṁ
dig-antaṁ cāmbaraṁ tathā 4.12.16

“Throwing fistfulls of red powder, they reddened the sky, the earth, and all the directions.”

koṭiśaḥ koṭiśas tatra
sphuranty ābīra-muṣṭayaḥ
su-gandhāruṇa-cūrṇānāṁ
koṭiśaḥ koṭiśas tathā 4.12.17

“There were millions and millions of reddish fists. Millions and millions of times they threw fragrant red powder.”

sarvato jagṛhuḥ kṛṣṇaṁ
karābhyāṁ vraja-gopikāḥ
yathā meghaṁ ca dāminyaḥ
sandhyāyāṁ śrāvaṇasya ca 4.12.18

“As lightning flashes surround a cloud in the month of Śrāvaṇa (July-August), so the vraja-gopīs surrounded Kṛṣṇa and held Him tightly with both hands.”

tan-mukhaṁ ca vilimpantyo
’thābīrāruṇa-muṣṭibhiḥ
kuṅkumākta-dṛtībhis taṁ
ardrī-cakrur vidhānataḥ 4.12.19

“With fistfulls of red powder they anointed His face. With sprinklers of kuṅuma-water they drenched Him.”

bhagavān api tatraiva
yāvatīr vraja-yoṣitaḥ
dhṛtvā rūpāṇi tāvanti
vijahāra nṛpeśvara 4.12.20

“O king of kings, then the Supreme Personality of Godhead expanded into as many forms as there were vraja-gopīs.”

rādhayā suśubhe tatra
holikāyā mahotsave
varṣāsandhyākṣane kṛṣṇaḥ
saudaminyā ghano yathā 4.12.21

“As a cloud touched by lightning in the rain, so Kṛṣṇa was splendidly manifest with Rādhā in the Holi festival.”

Chapter 13 - The Story of the Demigoddesses

atha devāṅganānāṁ ca
gopīnāṁ varṇanaṁ śṛṇu
catuṣpadārthadaṁ nṛṇāṁ
bhakti-vardhanam uttamam 4.13.1

“Now please hear the story of the demigoddesses that became gopīs, a story that grants the four goals of life and increases one’s love for Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

babhūva mālave deśe
gopo nando divas-patiḥ
bhāryā-sahasra-samyukto
dhanavān nītimān paraḥ 4.13.2

“In Mālava-deśa there was a great gopa-king named Nanda-divaspati. He was righteous and wealthy, and he had a thousand wives.”

tīrtha-yātrā-prasaṅgena
mathurāyāṁ samāgataḥ
nanda-rājaṁ vrajādhīśaṁ
śrutvā śrī-gokulaṁ yayau 4.13.3

“As he was going on pilgrimage, he arrived in Mathurā. When he heard of Vraja’s King Nanda, he went to Gokula.”

militvā gopa-rājaṁ sa
dṛṣṭvā vṛndāvana-śriyam
nanda-rājājñayā tatra
vāsaṁ cakre mahā-manāḥ 4.13.4

“He met King Nanda, and he saw the beauty of Vṛndāvana. With Nanda’s permission, the noble-hearted king made his residence there.”

yojana-dvayam āśritya
ghoṣaṁ cakre gavāṁ punaḥ
mudaṁ prāpa vraje rājan
jñātibhiḥ sa divas-patiḥ 4.13.5

“Having made a sixteen-mile place for his cows, King Divaspati and his relatives lived happily.”

tasya devala-vākyena
sarvā devajana-striyaḥ
jātāḥ kanyā mahā-divyā
jvalad-agni-śikhopmāḥ 4.13.6

“By Devala Muni’s words, all the demigoddesses were born as his daughters, daughters splendid like flames in a blazing fire.”

śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ sundaraṁ dṛṣṭvā
mohitāḥ kanyakāś ca tāḥ
dāmodarasya prāpty-arthaṁ
cakrur māgha-vrataṁ param 4.13.7

“When they saw handsome Kṛṣṇa they were bewildered with love for Him. To attain Him they performed the great vow of Māgha-vrata.”

ardhodaye ’rke yamunāṁ
nityaṁ snātvā vrajāṅganāḥ
uccair jaguḥ kṛṣṇa-līlāṁ
premānanda-samākulāḥ 4.13.8

“As they bathed at sunrise in the Yamunā, these girls of Vraja, who had fallen in love with Kṛṣṇa, every day loudly sang of Lord Kṛṣṇa’s pastimes.”

tāsāṁ prasannaḥ śrī-kṛṣṇo
varaṁ brūhīty uvāca ha
tā ūcus taṁ paraṁ natvā
kṛtāñjali-puṭāḥ śanaiḥ 4.13.9

“Pleased with them, Lord Kṛṣṇa said to them, “Please ask for a benediction.” Slowly bowing down with folded hands, these girls spoke to Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-gopya ūcuḥ
yogīśvarāṇāṁ kila durlabhas tvaṁ
sarveśvaraḥ kāraṇa-kāraṇo ’si
tvaṁ netra-gāmī bhāvatāt sadā no
vaṁsi-dharo manmatha-manmathāṅgaḥ 4.13.10

The gopīs said: “Even the great kings of the yogīs cannot attain You. You are the master of all, the first cause of all causes. Holding a flute, and more handsome than Kāmadeva, please always stay before our eyes.”

tathāstu coktvā harir ādi-devas
tāsāṁ tu yo darśanam ātatāna
bhūyāt sadā te hṛdi netra-mārge
tathā sa āhūta ivāśu citte 4.13.11

“Lord Kṛṣṇa said, “So be it.” He was always before them. He appeared in their heart and He appeared before their eyes as if they had just called Him.”

paripūrṇatamaḥ sākṣāc
chrī-kṛṣṇo nānya eva hi
eka-kāryārtham āgatya
koṭi-kāryaṁ cakāra ha 4.13.12

“Śrī Kṛṣṇa, and no one else, is the original Supreme Personality of Godhead. Coming to this world to do one thing, He did millions of other things as well.”

parikarī-kṛta-pīta-paṭaṁ hariṁ
śikhi-kirīṭa-natī-kṛta-kandharam
lakuṭa-veṇu-karaṁ cala-kuṇḍalaṁ
paṭutaraṁ naṭa-veṣa-dharaṁ bhaje 4.13.13

“I worship most intelligent Kṛṣṇa who, with a peacock-feather crown, swinging earrings, a yellow sash about His waist, and a stick and flute in His hand, is dressed as a graceful-shouldered dancer.”

Chapter 14 - The Story of the Jālandharīs

śrī-nārada uvāca
jālandharīṇāṁ gopīnāṁ
janmāni śṛṇu maithila
karmāṇi ca mahā-rāja
pāpa-ghnāni nṛṇāṁ sadā 4.14.1

Śrī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, please hear the story of the births and deeds of the Jālandharīs that became gopīs, a story that again and again removes the people’s sins.”

rājan sapta-nadī-tīre
raṅga-paṭṭanam uttamam
sarva-sampad-yutaṁ dīrghaṁ
yojana-dvaya-vartulam 4.14.2

“O king, on the shore of seven rivers there was a great city named Raṅga-paṭṭana. Sixteen miles around, it was filled with all opulences.”

raṅgojis tatra gopālaḥ
purādhīśo mahā-balaḥ
putra-pautra-samāyukto
dhana-dhānya-samṛddhimān 4.14.3

“A gopa named Raṅgoji was the king of that city. He was very powerful and wealthy, and He had many children and grandchildren”

hastināpura-nāthāya
dhṛtarāṣṭrāya bhūbhṛte
haimānām arbuda-śataṁ

“O king, once, at the year’s end, Raṅgoji, overcome with pride and foolishness, did not pay his annual tribute to emperor Dhṛtarāṣṭra.”

mīlanārthaṁ na cāyāte
raṅgojau gopa-nāyake
vīrā daśa sahasrāṇi
dhṛtarāṣṛta-praṇoditāḥ 4.14.6

“When the gopa-king Raṅgoji would not even go to meet him, emperor Dhṛtarāṣṭra sent an army of ten thousand soldiers.”

baddhvā taṁ dāmabhir gopaṁ

“They bound Raṅgoji with ropes and took him to Hastināpura. How many years was he in prison?”

san niruddhas tāḍito ’pi
lobhī bhīrur na cābhavat
na dadau sa dhanaṁ kiñcid
dhṛtarāṣṭrāya bhūbhṛte 4.14.8

“Although he was beaten in prison, he was unafraid. He was so greedy he still would not give any money to emperor Dhṛtarāṣṭra.”

kārāgārān mahā-bhīmāt
kadācit sa palāyitaḥ
rātrau raṅga-puraṁ prāgād
raṅgojir gopa-nāyakaḥ 4.14.9

“One night the gopa-king Raṅgoji escaped from the prison and returned to his own city.”

punas taṁ hi samāhartuṁ
dhṛtarāṣṭra-praṇoditam
akṣauhiṇī-trayaṁ rājan
samartha-bala-vāhanam 4.14.10

“Then Dhṛtarāṣṭra sent three akṣuahiṇī divisions to recapture Raṅgoji.”

tena sārdhaṁ hi bāṇaughais
tīkṣṇa-dhāraiḥ sphurat-prabhaiḥ
yuyudhe daṁśito yuddhe
dhanus taṅkārayan muhuḥ 4.14.11

“Although wounded by a flood of arrows, he continued to fight, again and again shooting his bow.”

śatrubhiś chinna-kavacas
chinna-dhanvā hata-svakaḥ
puram etya mṛdhaṁ cakre
raṅgojiḥ katibhir dinaiḥ 4.14.12

“Even after his enemies broke his armor and bow and killed his soldiers, for how many days did Raṅgoji fight?”

anāthaḥ śaraṇaṁ cecchan
kaṁsāya yadu-bhūbhṛte
dūtaṁ svaṁ preṣayām āsa
raṅgojir bhaya-pīḍitaḥ 4.14.13

“When all was hopeless, he sent a messenger to Kaṁsa, the king of the Yadus.”

dūtas tu mathurām etya
sabhāṁ gatvā natānanaḥ
kṛtāñjaliś caugraseniṁ
natvā prāha girārdrayā 4.14.14

“The messenger went to Mathurā, entered the royal assembly, with folded hands and bowed head offered obeisances to King Kaṁsa, and in a voice choked with emotion spoke the following words.”

raṅgoji-nāma nṛpa raṅga-paṭṭane
gopo ’sti nītijñā-varaḥ purādhipaḥ
sva-śatru-samruddha-puro mahādhi-bhṛd
alabdha-nāthaḥ śaraṇaṁ gatas tava 4.14.15

“O king, a righteous gopa named Raṅgoji is the ruler of the city Raṅga-paṭṭana. His army defeated, he is now distressed and without a shelter. Now he takes shelter of you.”

tvaṁ dīna-duḥkhārti-haro mahī-tale
bhaumādi-saṅgīta-guṇo mahā-balaḥ

“You remove the troubles of they whgo suffer in this world. Your virtues are sung everywhere. Having defeated the demigods and demons, you rule the earth. You are like Indra, the king of the demigods.”

candraṁ cakoraś ca raviṁ kuśeśayaṁ

kṣudhāturo ’nnaṁ ca jalaṁ tṛṣāturaḥ
smaraty asau śatru-bhaye tathā tvām 4.14.17

“As a cakora bird thinks of the moon, as a lotus flower thinks of the sun, as a cātaka bird thinks of the autumn rains, as a hungry man thinks of food, and as a thirsty man thinks of water, so King Raṅgoji, frightened in battle, thinks of you.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
itthaṁ śrutvā vacas tasya
kaṁso vai dīna-vatsalaḥ
daitya-koṭi-samāyukto
mano gantuṁ samādadhe 4.14.18

Śrī Nārada said: “After hearing these words, Kaṁsa, the friend of the distressed, decided to go there with ten million demons.”

go-mūtra-caya-sindūra-
kastūrī-patra-bhṛn mukham
vindhyādri-sādṛśaṁ śyāmaṁ
mada-nirjhara-samyutam
pade ca śṛṅkhalājālaṁ
nadantaṁ ghanavad bhṛśām
dvīpaṁ kuvalayāpīḍaṁ
samāruhya madotkaṭaḥ 4.14.19-20

“Burning with passion, Kaṁsa climbed on Kuvalayāpīḍa elephant, who was black and enormous like the Vindhyā mountains, who gushed with streams of rut, whose face was decorated with designs drawn with gomūtra, musk, and red sindūra, whose feet were covered with a network of chains, and who trumpeted like thunder.”

cāṇūra-muṣṭikādyaiś ca
keśi-vyoma-vṛṣāsuraiḥ
sahasā daṁśitaḥ kaṁsaḥ
prāyayau raṅga-paṭṭanam 4.14.21

“Accompanied by Cāṇūra, Muṣṭika, Keśī, Vyoma, Ariṣṭāsura, and a host of other demons, Kaṁsa quickly traveled to Raṅga-paṭṭana.”

yadūnāṁ ca kurūṇāṁ ca
balayos tu parasparam
bāṇaiḥ khaḍgais triśūlaiś ca
ghoraṁ yuddhaṁ babhūva ha 4.14.22

“Armed with arrows, swords, and tridents, the armies of the Yadus and Kurus fought a gory battle.”

bāṇāndhakāre sañjāte
kaṁso nītvā mahā-gadam
viveśa kuru-senāsu
vane vaiśvānaro yathā 4.14.23

“The sky became dark with a great shower of arrows. Then, as a fire enters a forest, so Kaṁsa, armed with a great club, penetrated the Kuru army.”

kāṁścid vīrān sa-kavacān
gadayā vajra-kalpayā
pātayām āsa bhū-pṛṣṭhe
vajreṇendro yathā girim 4.14.24

“As King Indra with his thunderbolt knocks down a great mountain, so Kaṁsa with his thunderbolt club, knocked many armored warriors to the ground.”

rathān mamarda pādābhyāṁ
paṛṣṇi-ghātena ghoṇṭakān
gaje gajaṁ tāḍayitvā
gajān pronnīya cāṅghriṣu 4.14.25

“Kicking them with his feet, Kaṁsa destroyed many chariots. Kicking them with his heel, Kaṁsa killed many horses. He beat many elephants and many he overturned, their feet kicking in the air.”

skandhayoḥ kakṣayor dhṛtvā
sa-nīḍān ratna-kambalān
kaṁścid balād bhrāmayitvā
cikṣepa gagane balī 4.14.26

“Grabbing them at the root of their forelegs, powerful Kaṁsa picked up many jewel-saddled elephants, whirled them around, and threw them into the sky.”

gajāñ chuṇḍāsu connīya
lola-ghaṇṭā-samāvṛtān
cikṣepa sammukhe rājan
mṛdhe vyomāsuro balī 4.14.27

“O king, Vyomāsura picked up many bell-decorated elephants and threw them in the faces of the enemy army.”

rathān gṛhitvā sāśvāṁś ca
śṛṅgābhyāṁ bhrāmayan muhuḥ
cikṣepa dikṣu balavān
daityo duṣṭo vṛṣāsuraḥ 4.14.28

“In its horns the powerful demon Ariṣṭāsura picked up many great chariots and their horses, whirled them around, and threw them in all directions.”

balāt paścima-pādābhyāṁ
vīrān aśvān itas tataḥ
pātayām āsa rājendra
keśī daityādhipo balī 4.14.29

“O king of kings, kicking them with its hind legs, the great demon Keśī threw many powerful horses to the ground.”

evaṁ bhayaṅkaraṁ yuddhaṁ
dṛṣṭvā vai kuru-sainikāḥ
śeṣā bhayāturā vīrā
jagmus te ’pi diśo daśa 4.14.30

“When they saw the fearsome nature of this battle, the remaining Kuru soldiers became frightened and fled in the ten directions.”

raṅgojiṁ sa-kuṭumbaṁ taṁ
nītvā kaṁso ’tha daitya-rāṭ
mathurāṁ prāyayau vīro
nādayan dundubhīn chanaiḥ 4.14.31

“Taking Raṅgoji and his family with him, the demon-king Kaṁsa triumphantly entered Mathurā as the drums played a slow march.”

śrutvā parājayaṁ svasya
kauravāḥ krodha-murchitāḥ
daityānāṁ samayaṁ dṛṣṭvā
sarve vai maunam āsthitāḥ 4.14.32

“Hearing of their own defeat, the Kurus were overcome with anger. Seeing that things were now favorable for the demons, everyone became silent.”

puraṁ barhiṣadaṁ nāma
vraja-sīmni manoharam
raṅgojaye dadau kaṁso
daityānām adhipo balī 4.14.33

“Then the powerful demon-king Kaṁsa gave to Raṅgoji a beautiful city named Barhiṣat, which stood on the outskirts of Vraja.”

vāsaṁ cakāra tatraiva
raṅgojir gopa-nāyakaḥ
babhūvus tasya bhāryāsu
jālandharyo harer varāt 4.14.34

“The gopa-king Raṅgoji made his home there. There, by Lord Kṛṣṇa’s benediction, the Jālandharīs were born from his many wives.”

pariṇītā gopa-janai
rūpa-yauvana-bhūṣitāḥ
jāra-dharmeṇa su-snehaṁ
śrī-kṛṣṇe tāḥ pracakrire 4.14.35

“Decorated with beauty and youth, they were married to various gopas. They loved Lord Kṛṣṇa as their paramour.”

Chapter 15 - The Story of the Women of Barhiṣmatī-pura, the Apsarās, and the Women of Sutala and Nāgendra

śrī-nārada uvāca
vraje śoṇapurādhiśo
gopo nando dhanī mahān
bhāryāḥ pañca sahasrāṇi
babhūvus tasya maithila 4.15.1

Śrī Nārada said: “O king of Mithilā, in Vraja lived a righteous and wealthy gopa named Nanda, who had been the king of Śoṇapura. He had five thousand wives.”

jātā matsya-varāt tās tu
samudre gopa-kanyakāḥ
tathānyāś ca vicitrāḥ
pṛthivyā dohanān nṛpa 4.15.2

“He had many daughters. Some had previously been born from the milk-ocean and had been the goddess of fortune’s friends, and now were born as gopīs by Lord Matsya’s benediction, and others had been the plants the sprouted when the earth was milked.”

barhiṣmatī-pur-andhryo yā
jātā jāti-smarāḥ parāḥ
tathānyāpsaraso ’bhūvan
varān nārāyaṇasya ca 4.15.3

“Some had been the women of Barhiṣmatī-pura. They remembered their previous birth. Others had been apsarās and became gopī by the blessings of Nārāyaṇa Ṛṣi”

tathā sutala-vāsinyo
vāmanasya varāt striyaḥ
tathā nāgendra-kanyāś ca
jātāḥ śeṣa-varāt parāt 4.15.4

“Some had been the women of Sutalaloka and became gopīs by the blessings of Lord Vāmana. Others had been the daughters of the king of the Nāgas and they became gopīs by the spiritual blessings of Lord Śeṣa.”

tābhyo durvāsasā dattaṁ
kṛṣṇā-pañcāṅgam adbhutam
tena sampūjya yamunāṁ
vavrire śrīpatiṁ varam 4.15.5

“Durvāsā Muni gave them the wonderful kṛṣṇā-pañcāṅga method of worship. Following it, they worshiped the Yamunā and attained Lord Kṛṣṇa as their husband.”

ekadā śrī-haris tābhir
vṛndāraṇye manohare
yamunā-nikaṭe divye
puṁs-kokila-taru-vraje
madhupa-dhvani-samyukte
kūjat-kokila-sārase
madhu-māse manda-vāyau
vasanta-latikāvṛte
dolotsavaṁ samārebhe
harir madana-mohanaḥ
kadamba-vrkṣe rahasi
kalpa-vrkṣe manohare 4.15.6-8

“Once, during the month of Caitra (March-April), Lord Kṛṣṇa, who is more charming than Kāmadeva, enjoyed a swing festival with these gopīs in a kadamba kalpa-vṛkṣa tree in a beautiful and secluded place in beautiful Vṛndāvana forest, a place filled with trees and mādhavī vines, buzzing bees, cooing cuckoos and cranes, and a gentle breeze from the nearby Yamunā.”

kālindī-jala-kallola-
kolāhala-samākule
tad-dola-khelanaṁ cakrus
tā gopyaḥ prema-vihvalāḥ 4.15.9

“As the Yamunā was filled with waves, these gopīs, deeply in love with Kṛṣṇa, enjoyed pastimes on a swing.”

rādhayā kīrti-sutayā
candra-koṭi-prakāśayā
reje vṛndāvane kṛṣṇo
yathā ratyā ratīśvaraḥ 4.15.10

“As Kāmadeva shines with Rati by His side, so in Vṛndāvana forest Lord Kṛṣṇa shone with Kīrti’s daughter Rādhā, who was more beautiful and glorious than millions of moons, by His side.”

evaṁ prāptāś ca yāḥ sarvāḥ
śrī-kṛṣṇaṁ nanda-nandanam
paripūrṇatamaṁ sākṣāt
tāsāṁ kiṁ varṇyate tapaḥ 4.15.11

“All these gopīs attained Nanda’s son, Lord Kṛṣṇa, the perfect Supreme Personality of Godhead, as their lover. Who can describe the austerities they must have performed?”

nāgendra-kanyā yāḥ sarvās
caitra-māse manohare
balabhadraṁ hariṁ prāptāḥ
kṛṣṇā-tīre tu tāḥ śubhāḥ 4.15.12

“During the beautiful month of Caitra (March-April), by the Yamunā’s shore, the beautiful gopīs that had been the daughters of the Nāga-king attained the Supreme Personality of Godhead, Lord Balarāma, as their lover.”

idaṁ mayā te kathitaṁ
gopīnāṁ caritaṁ śubham
sarva-pāpa-haraṁ puṇyaṁ kiṁ bhūyaḥ śrotum icchasi 4.15.13

“Now I have told you the beautiful and sacred story of the gopīs, a story that removes all sins. What more do you wish to hear?”

śrī-bahulāśva uvāca
yamunāyāś ca pañcāṅgaṁ
dattaṁ durvāsasā mune
gopībhyo yena govindaḥ
prāptas tad brūhi māṁ prabho 4.15.14

Śrī Bahulāśva said: “O sage, please describe to me the pañcāṅga method of worshiping the Yamunā that Durvāsā gave to the gopīs and that they followed to attain Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-nārada uvāca
atraivodaharantīmaṁ
itihāsaṁ purātanam
yasya śravaṇa-mātreṇa
pāpa-hāniḥ parā bhavet 4.15.15

Śrī Nārada said: “Simply by hearing this ancient story one becomes free of all sins.”

ayodhyādhipatiḥ śrīmān
mandhātā rāja-sattamaḥ
mṛgayāṁ vicaran prāptaḥ
saubharer āśramaṁ śubham
vṛndāvane sthitaṁ sākṣāt
kṛṣṇā-tīre manohare
natvā jāmātaraṁ rājā
saubhariṁ prāha mānadaḥ 4.15.16-17

“As glorious Mandhātā, the king of Ayodhyā, was hunting, he wandered into Saubhari Muni’s auspicious āśrama, which was in beautiful Vṛndāvana forest by the Yamunā’s shore. Bowing down before his son-in-law, the respectful king spoke to Saurabhi.”

śrī-mandhātovāca
bhagavan sarva-vit sākṣāt
tvaṁ parāvara-vittamaḥ
lokānāṁ tamaso ’ndhānāṁ
divya-sūrya ivāparaḥ 4.15.18

Śrī Mandhātā said: “O Lord, O best of the all-knowing, You are like a second sun that lights up the darkness of the worlds.”

iha loke bhāved rājyaṁ
sarva-siddhi-samanvitam
amutra kṛṣṇa-sarūpyaṁ
yena syāt tad vadāśu me 4.15.19

“Please tell me how in this life I may attain a great kingdom and in the next life a spiritual form like that of Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

śrī-saubharir uvāca
yamunāyāś ca pañcāṅgaṁ
vadiṣyāmi tavāgrataḥ
sarva-siddhi-karaṁ śaśvat
kṛṣṇa-sārūpya-kārakam 4.15.20

Śrī Saubhari said: “I will describe to you the pañcāṅga method of worshiping the Yamunā. This worship brings all perfection. It gives one a spiritual form like that of Lord Kṛṣṇa.”

yāvat sūrya udeti sma
yāvac ca pratitiṣṭhati
tāvad rājya-pradaṁ cātra
śrī-kṛṣṇa-vaśa-kārakam 4.15.21

“This worship brings one a kingdom that lasts for as long as the sun rises and shines in the sky. This worship makes Lord Kṛṣṇa into one’s submissive servant.”

Chapter 16 - The Srī Yamunā Armor

śrī-mandhātovāca yamunāyāḥ krsna-rājnyāḥ
kavacaṁ sarvato ’malam dehi mahyaṁ mahā-bhāga dhārayisyāmy ahaṁ sadā 4.16.1

Srī Mandhātā said: “O fortunate one, please give to me the supremely splendid kavaca of Srī Yamunā, Lord Krsna’s queen. I will always keep in very carefully.”

śrī-caurabhir uvāca yamunāyāc ca kavacam sarva-raksakaram nrnām catus-padārtha-dam sāksāc chrnu rājan mahā-mate 4.16.2

Srī saurabhi said: “O noble-hearted king, please hear the Yamunā-kavaca, which protects one from all dangers and brings to one the four goals of life.”

krsnām catur-bhujām cyāmām puṇḍarīka-daleksanām ratha-sthām sundarīm dhyātvā dhārayet kavacam tataḥ 4.16.3

“One should meditate on dark, four-armed, beautiful, lotus-petal-eyed Yamunā, who is seated on a chariot. Then one should meditate on the Yamunā-kavaca.”

snātaḥ purva-mukho maunī krta-sandhyaḥ kucāsane kucair baddha-cikho vipraḥ
pathed vai svastikāsanaḥ 4.16.4

“A brāhmana should bathe, face the east, refrain from conversation, offer the sandhyā prayers, sit in the svastika-āsana posture on a kuca mat, tie his hair with kuca grass, and recite the following words.”

yamunā me ciraḥ pātu krsnā netra-dvayam sadā cyāmā bhru-bhagga-decam ca nāsikām nāka-vāsinī 4.16.5

“May Yamunā always protect my head. May Krsnā always protect my eyes. May Syāmā always protect my eyebrows. May Nākavāsinī always protect my nose.”

kapolau pātu me sāksāt paramānanda-rupinī krsna-vāmāmsa-sambhutā pātu karna-dvayam mama 4.16.6 adharau pātu kālindī cibukam surya-kanyakā yama-svasā kandharam ca hrdayam me mahā-nadī 4.16.7

“May Kālindī always protect my lips. May Surya-kanyakā always protect my chin. May Yama-svasā always protect my neck. May Mahā-nadī always protect my chest.”

krsna-priyā pātu prsthim tatinī me bhuja-dvayam cronī-tatam ca su-cronī katim me cāru-darcanā krsna 4.16.8

“May Krsna-priyā always protect my back. May Tatinī always protect my arms. May Sucronī always protect my loins. May Cāru-darcanā always protect my hips.”

uru-dvayam tu rambhorur jānunī tv agghri-bhedinī gulphau rāsecvarī pātu pādau pāpa-prahārinī uru 4.16.9

“May Rambhoru always protect my thighs. May Agghri-bhedinī always protect my knees. May Rāsecvarī always protect my ankles. May Pāpa-prahārinī always protect my feet.”

antar bahir adhac cordhvam dicāsu vidicāsu ca samantāt pātu jagataḥ 4.16.10

“May Paripurnatama-priyā always protect me from everything that is inside, outside, above, below, in the eight directions, and in the entire universe.”

idam śrī-yamunāyāc ca kavacam paramādbhutam daca-vāram pathed bhaktyā nirdhano dhanavān bhavet 4.16.11

“A pauper who ten times recites with devotion this very wonderful Yamunā-kavaca becomes wealthy.”

tribhir māsaiḥ pathed dhīmān brahmacārī mitācanaḥ
sarva-rājyādhipatyam ca prāpyate nātra samcayaḥ 4.16.12

“A wise brahmacārī who eats little and for three months recites this kavaca attains a great kingdom. Of this there is no doubt.”

dacottara-catam nityam tri-māsāvadhi bhaktitaḥ 4.16.13

“What is not attained by a person who within three months chants this kavaca 110 times with devotion?”

Chapter 17 - Prayers to Srī Yamunā

śrī-mandhātovāca yamunāyāḥ stavam divyam sarva-siddhi-karam param saubhare muni-cārdula vada mā krpayā tvaram śrī-mandhātā 4.17.1

Srī Mandhātā said: “O Saubhari, O tiger among sages, please kindly tell me the transcendental Yamunā-stava, which brings all perfection.”

śrī-saubharir uvāca mārtaṇḍa-kanyakāyās tu stavam crnu mahā-mate sarva-siddhi-karam bhumau catur-vargya-phala-pradam 4.17.2

Srī Saubhari said: “O noble-hearted one, please hear the Yamunā-stava, which grants all perfection and brings the four goals of life.”

krsna-vāmāmsa-bhutāyai krsnāyai satatam namaḥ
namaḥ śrī-krsna-rupinyai krsne tubhyam namo namaḥ 4.17.3

iti ciksām gatā gopā harsitā maunam āsthitāḥ
manasy ucur ayam krsna ucca-simhāsane sthitaḥ 4.22.17

“Hearing these words of instruction, the gopas were happy and silent. In their hearts they said to themselves, “Our Krsna is seated on a great throne!”

asmān durād adhaḥ krtvā-smābhir vakti na karhicit tasmād vrajād varam nāsti ko ’pi loko na saukhya-daḥ 4.22.18

“He is up above, and we are way below. He does not speak to us a single word. From this we can understand that no place is better than Vraja. No place is happier than Vraja.”

Chapter 23 - The Story of Sudarcana

śri-nārada uvāca ekadā nrpa gopālāḥ
cakatai ratna-puritaiḥ 4.23.1

“One day the gopas, headed by Upananda and King Vrsabhānu, took many carts filled with jewels to Ambikāvana forest.”

bhadrakālim pacupatim pujayitvā vidhānataḥ
dadur dānam dvijātibhyaḥ 4.23.2

“After worshiping Lord Siva and goddess Durgā, and after giving charity to the brāhmanas, they slept on by the riverbank there.”

tatraiko nirgato rātrau sarpo nandam pade ’grahit krsna krsneti cukroca nando ’ti-bhaya-vihvalaḥ 4.23.3

“In the middle of the night a snake came and grabbed Nanda’s feet. Terrified, Nanda cried out, “Krsna! Krsna!”

tadolmukair gopa-bālās toḍur ājagaram nrpa padam so ’pi na tatyāja sarpo ’tha sva-manim yathā 4.23.4

“The gopa boys beat the snake with torches, but it would not leave Nanda any more than it would leave the jewel growing from its head.”

tatāḍa sva-padā sarpam bhagavāl loka-pāvanaḥ 4.23.5

“Then the Supreme Personality of Godhead, the purifier of the worlds, kicked the snake. That soul at once left his snake body and became a pious vidyādhara. With folded hands the vidyādhara bowed before Lord Krsna, circumambulated Him, and then spoke.”

śri-sudarcana uvāca aham sudarcano nāma vidyādhara-varaḥ prabho astāvakram munim drstvā hasito ’smi mahā-balaḥ 4.23.6

Sri Sudarcana said: “My name is Sudarcana. I am a great vidyādhara. When I saw the sage Astāvakra, I laughed at him.”

mahyam cāpam dadau so ’pi tvam sarpo bhava durmate tac-chāpād adya mukto ’ham krpayā tava mādhava 4.23.7

“He cursed me, “Fool! Now become a snake!” O Krsna, by Your mercy I am today freed from that curse.”

tvat-pāda-padma-makaranda-rajaḥ-kanānām sparcena divya-padavim sahasāgato ’smi tasmai namo bhagavate bhuvanecvarāya yo bhuri-bhāra-haranāya bhuvo ’vatāraḥ 4.23.8

“The touch of the the nectar pollen of Your lotus feet has made me a demigod again. I offer my respectful obeisances to You, the Supreme Personality of Godhead, the master of the worlds, who has desceneded to this world to relieve the earth of its burden.”

śri-nārada uvāca iti natvā harim krsnam rājan vidyādharas tu saḥ 4.23.9

Sri Nārada said: “O king, after bowing down before Lord Krsna, the vidyādhara departed for the spiritual world of Visnuloka.”

nandādyāḥ vismitāḥ sarve jnātvā krsnam parecvaram ambikāvanataḥ cighram āyayur vraja-maṇḍalam nanda 4.23.10

“Astonished, and now acutely aware of Krsna’s divinity, Nanda and the gopas left Ambikāvana and quickly returned to the circle of Vraja.”

idam mayā te kathitam śri-krsna-caritam cubham sarva-pāpa-haram punyam kim bhuyaḥ crotum icchasi 4.23.11

“Now I have described to you Sri Krsna’s beautiful and sacred pastimes, which remove all sins. What more do you wish to hear?”

śri-bahulācva uvāca aho śri-krsnacandrasya caritam paramādbhutam crutvā mano me tac chrotum atrptam punar icchati 4.23.12

Sri Bahulācva said: “My heart never tires of hearing Lord Krsnacandra’s very wonderful pastimes. It yearns to hear them again and again.”
